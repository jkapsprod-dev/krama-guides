# Live run sheet on the day

**What it is:** Your run sheet, turned into a live checklist. You tick each item as it happens, and Krama records the real time next to the plan.
**Why you'd use it:** The pheras start 40 minutes late. One tap tells the pandit, the decorator and the door team the new times.

Ticks switch on 6 hours before a function starts and stay on until 6 hours after it ends. Before that, you see a plain run sheet to read.

## How to tick items as they happen
1. Open **Event day**, tap the function, then tap the **Run sheet** tab. You can also use **Run sheets** in the menu.
2. When an item begins, tap **Start**.
3. When it ends, tap **Done**.
4. Made a mistake? Tap **Not started** or **Not done** to take the tick back.

**Tip:** A gold line marked **Now** shows where you are on the list.
**Stuck?** No **Start** button? Helpers can tick only the items given to them. Hosts, planners and family can tick any item.

## How to mark the key moment
1. Open the **Run sheet** tab.
2. Find the item that matters most, like the pheras or the varmala.
3. Tap **Key moment** on that item.
4. To remove the star, tap **Not the key moment**.

**Tip:** Only one item holds the star. The recap later shows its planned time and its real time.
**Stuck?** No **Key moment** button? Only hosts, planners and family see it.

## How to tell everyone you are running late
1. Tap **Running late…**.
2. Pick how long: 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Or type your own number of minutes.
3. Under **Move items from**, keep **Now (everything not started yet)**, or pick an item.
4. Keep **Tell members, linked vendors and door staff** ticked.
5. Check the line that says how many items will move. Then tap **Move {n} min later**.
6. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo**.

**Tip:** Before the function starts, you can also tick the box to move the function's own start time. Guests are told only if the **Function time changed** automation is on.
**Stuck?** The button says **Running late needs the network**? You are offline. Try again when the signal is back.
