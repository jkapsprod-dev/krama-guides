# Counts

**What it is:** The numbers you need to order things. Plates, mithai, return gifts, invitation cards, chairs, tables, bus seats and rooms, all worked out from who is coming.
**Why you'd use it:** The caterer asks how many plates, and the sweet shop asks how many kilos of mithai. You have the answer in one tap.

The numbers are worked out fresh each time you open the page. Nothing is saved, so they never go out of date. Everyone in the wedding can read them. They never show money.

## How to see the counts for a function or the whole wedding
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function.
2. Under **On the day**, tap **See the counts**.
3. Read each line. It shows the number and how it was worked out.
4. For the whole wedding, go back to **Functions** and tap **Counts for the whole wedding**.

**Tip:** In the whole-wedding view, each person is counted once. Chairs and tables are for the biggest function, because they are set up one function at a time.
**Stuck?** Plates show **Nobody has said yes yet**? Guests have not replied. Send reminders from **Guests**.

## How to change the ratios
1. Open the counts page.
2. Find **Ratios** on the page.
3. Change a number, like **Mithai a person (grams)** or **Seats a table**.
4. Tap **Save ratios**.
5. To go back, tap **Use the usual numbers**.

**Tip:** The usual number shows under each box, so you can see what you changed.
**Stuck?** The boxes are not there? Only hosts and planners can change ratios. Each ratio must be a whole number within the limits shown.

## How to print or download the counts
1. Open the counts page.
2. Tap **Print** for paper.
3. Or tap **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet.

**Tip:** Send the CSV to your caterer, or keep it with your own notes.
**Stuck?** No **Download CSV** button? Hosts, planners and family can download. Others can read and print.
