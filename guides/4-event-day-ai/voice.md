# Voice (speak instead of typing)

**What it is:** A microphone button that turns your voice into text. You can speak to the assistant, or speak your meeting notes.
**Why you'd use it:** Your hands are full of marigold garlands. You say "Open Money" instead of typing it.

**Add-on, BETA:** Voice comes with the assistant add-on. If the microphone doesn't work, the add-on may be off. A host can check it in **Settings**, then **Plan & add-ons**.

## How to speak instead of typing
1. Tap **Ask the assistant**. Find the microphone button next to the box.
2. Tap the microphone and speak. The screen says **Listening… tap again to stop (up to a minute)**.
3. Tap **Stop recording** when you finish.
4. Wait for **Writing down what you said…**. Your words appear in the box.
5. Check the words, then tap **Send**.

**Tip:** You can use the same microphone on the **From a meeting** page, to speak your notes.
**Stuck?** The microphone is blocked? Allow it in your browser settings. A recording can last up to one minute.

## How to say a command to the assistant
1. Tap the microphone in the assistant and say a command, like "Open Money".
2. Tap **Send**.
3. Tap the link that appears, like **Open Money**. It opens that screen.

**Tip:** Commands only open a screen or a form. They never change your wedding. Try "Open Guests", "Open Tasks", "Open the run sheet", "Add a guest" or "Add an expense".
**Stuck?** Nothing opens? Say or type "open" and the name of the screen.

## How to turn a voice note into tasks or arrivals
1. Speak or type a note, like "Mama ji arrives Friday 6 pm".
2. Tap **Turn into actions**.
3. Read the list. Each item says **New task** or **New arrival**.
4. Tap **Review and save** on an item. Check the form, then save it.

**Tip:** Nothing is saved until you check it and save it yourself.
**Stuck?** It says it couldn't find a task or an arrival? Try "add a task to call the caterer" or "Mama ji arrives Friday 6 pm". Only hosts, planners and family can turn notes into changes.
