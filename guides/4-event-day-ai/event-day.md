# Event day

**What it is:** One screen on your phone for the day itself. The run sheet, check-in, emergency card, seating, crew, contacts and food all sit in one place, and it keeps working with no signal.
**Why you'd use it:** The hall's Wi-Fi drops just as the baraat arrives, and Mausi still wants to know where her table is.

## How to open event-day mode
1. Open **Event day**. On a phone, it is under **More**. Helpers see it on the bottom bar.
2. Tap the function you are running. Today's functions come first.
3. Wait for the strip at the top. It says **Saved on this phone. Works with no network.**
4. You can also start from a function's page. Under **On the day**, tap **Open event-day mode**.

**Tip:** Open it at home or at the venue gate, while you still have signal. That is when the phone saves its copy.
**Stuck?** If the strip says **Keep this page open**, this browser can't reopen the page with no network. Don't close the tab.

## How to move between the tabs
1. Look at the row of buttons near the top: **Run sheet**, **Check-in**, **Emergency**, **Seating**, **Crew**, **Contacts** and **Food**.
2. Tap a button to open that part of the day.
3. In **Seating**, type in **Find a family** to see their table.
4. In **Contacts**, tap a phone number to call.

**Tip:** On a computer, **Run sheet** and **Check-in** sit side by side.
**Stuck?** A tab is missing? Some tabs show only for some people. A helper sees **Check-in** only after a host puts them on the door. Money never shows here.

## How to keep working without signal
1. Open event-day mode once while you have signal, and wait for the strip to say it is saved.
2. If the signal drops, a strip says **Offline** with the time of the last update. Keep going.
3. Ticks on the run sheet and check-ins wait on the phone.
4. When the signal is back, they send by themselves.

**Tip:** Stay on this page. Every tab inside it keeps working, so you never need to load another page.
**Stuck?** Running late needs the network, because it tells people. Try it again when you are back online.

## How to end event-day mode
1. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
2. Read the line that says when this copy leaves the phone. It leaves 12 hours after the function ends.
3. Tap **End event-day mode on this phone**.
4. If some changes were not sent yet, the app asks before it ends. Wait for signal first if you can.

**Tip:** End it on any phone that is not yours, like a helper's borrowed phone.
**Stuck?** Ended by mistake? Open **Event day** and the function again while you have signal. It makes a fresh copy.
