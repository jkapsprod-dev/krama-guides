# Seating

**What it is:** A table plan for one function. You add tables, seat each family, and print cards for the day.
**Why you'd use it:** Mama ji must sit near the stage, and the two sides should not end up arguing over one table.

Hosts, planners and family can change the plan. Viewers can only read it. Helpers can't open it, but they see a family's table on the check-in card.

## How to add tables
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function. Then open the **Seating** tab.
2. Under **Add tables**, type **How many**.
3. Set **Seats** for each table, and pick a **Shape**: **Round** or **Long**.
4. Tap **Add**.

**Tip:** To rename a table, tap its edit button and change **Name or number**. Then tap **Save**.
**Stuck?** Deleted a table by mistake? The families at it go back to the list. Seat them again.

## How to seat a family
1. In the list on the left, type in **Find a family**.
2. Tap the family's name.
3. On the table you want, tap **Seat {name} here**.
4. To move someone, tap the remove button on their table. They go back to the list.

**Tip:** On a computer, you can also drag a family onto a table. Each table shows how many seats are used, like "6 of 10 seats".
**Stuck?** A table says **more than seats**? You put more people than seats there. Move a family to another table.

## How to seat elders near the front
1. Open the function's **Seating** tab.
2. In the list of families waiting, find the family. Type in **Find a family** if the list is long.
3. Tap the arrow button beside the family's name. Its label is **Near the front: {name}**. It stays lit when it is on.
4. Do the same for every family with elders.
5. Tap **Suggest seats**. These families go to the first tables.

**Tip:** Families with children or infants also go to the front by themselves. You only need to mark the elders.
**Stuck?** No arrow button? It shows only for families still waiting for a seat, and only if you can change the plan.

## How to let Krama suggest seats
1. Tap **Suggest seats**.
2. Read the **Suggested plan**. Elders and children go to the first tables, and each side stays together where it can.
3. Happy with it? Tap **Use this plan**. Nothing changes until you do.
4. Not happy? Tap **Undo** to bring back the old seating.

**Tip:** Lock a table with **Lock table {label}** and the suggestion leaves it alone.
**Stuck?** The plan says some people have no seat? Add a table and tap **Suggest seats** again.

## How to print seating
1. Open the **Seating** tab and tap **Print seating**.
2. Look at the three parts on the page: **Table by table**, **Find your table** and **Table cards**.
3. Tap **Print**.

**Tip:** Put **Find your table** at the entrance, and the big **Table cards** on the tables.
**Stuck?** No **Print seating** link? It shows only after you add at least one table.
