# Plan & add-ons

**What it is:** A page that shows what is switched on for your wedding, and how much of each thing you have used.
**Why you'd use it:** Your aunty asks why the assistant button is missing on her phone. You check here whether the add-on is on.

Some features are add-ons, like the assistant, voice and meeting notes. They show on this page as **On** or **Off**. Right now everything is free during the pilot, so the page says **Free during the pilot**. Buying is not open yet, so this guide covers only how to look.

## How to see your plan and what is included
1. Open **Settings**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Tap **Plan & add-ons**.
3. Read the status at the top. It says **Free during the pilot** when everything is on at no cost.
4. Scroll to the list of add-ons. Each one says **On** or **Off**.
5. Look at **Usage** to see how much you have used, like **Assistant questions today**.

**Tip:** Check this page first when a feature is missing for someone. Add-ons are switched on for the whole wedding, not for one person.
**Stuck?** Can't open **Settings**? Only hosts and planners see it in the menu. Ask a host.
