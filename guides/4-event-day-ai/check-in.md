# Check-in

**What it is:** A door screen to welcome each family as they arrive. You scan their pass or find their name. Krama counts who is in.
**Why you'd use it:** Fifty cousins reach the reception at once. Your helper at the door needs to find "Mehta family" in two seconds, and you need to know how many have come.

## How to put a helper on the door
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function.
2. Open the **Check-in** tab.
3. Under **Helpers on the door**, find your helper.
4. Tap **Put on door**. The button now says **On the door**.

**Tip:** Hosts, planners and family can always check guests in. You only need to do this for helpers.
**Stuck?** No helpers in the list? Invite them first in **Members**. The list says **No helpers in this wedding** when there are none.

## How to check a family in
1. Open the function's **Check-in** tab, and tap **Open check-in**. Helpers can use **Event day** and the **Check-in** tab.
2. Tap **Scan pass** and point the camera at the family's QR pass. Or tap **Find family** and type the name under **Family name**.
3. Tap the family.
4. Check the number under **People arriving now**. Use **One fewer** or **One more** to match who is really here.
5. Tap **Check in {count}**.

**Tip:** The top of the screen shows **{arrived} of {expected} arrived**, so you always know the count. With no signal, check-ins are saved on the phone and sent later.
**Stuck?** The camera is blocked? Allow it in your browser settings, or use **Find family** instead.

## How to fix a mistake
1. Look under **Latest arrivals** for the family.
2. Tap **Undo** next to their name.
3. Check them in again if needed.

**Tip:** If someone checked in by mistake and signal is off, **Undo** waits until you are back online.
**Stuck?** The app says that check-in can't be undone any more? Ask a host. Hosts, planners and family can undo any check-in.
