# Emergency card

**What it is:** One card per function with the nearest hospital, the nearest police station, fire exits, first aid, and who to call.
**Why you'd use it:** A guest feels faint in the heat of the pheras. Nobody should have to hunt for the hospital's number.

Everyone in the wedding can read the card. Hosts, planners and family fill it in. Phone numbers are hidden from helpers and viewers on screen.

## How to fill in the emergency card
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function.
2. Under **On the day**, tap **Open card**. You can also use the **Emergency** tab.
3. Scroll to **Fill in the card**.
4. Under **Nearest hospital**, add the name, phone and address. Do the same for **Nearest police station**.
5. Fill in **Fire exits**, **First aid** and **First-aid kit carried by**.
6. Tap **Save card**.

**Tip:** Tap **Find a hospital near the venue**. It opens a map search, so you can copy the name and phone. The national number, 112, is already on the card.
**Stuck?** The phone number is not accepted? Use a full number, or a short code like 112.

## How to add the people to call
1. On the card, find **People to call**.
2. Tap the **Contact** box and pick a person under **Choose a contact**.
3. Under **What they are for**, pick a role, like **Venue manager** or **Electrician**.
4. Tap **Add a person from Contacts**.

**Tip:** Add the venue manager and the electrician. They are the first calls when the lights go out.
**Stuck?** The list says **There is nobody in Contacts yet**? Tap **Open Contacts** and add them first.

## How to print the card
1. Open the emergency card.
2. Tap **Print (A5)**.
3. Tap **Print** on the next page.

**Tip:** Put one printed card at the door and one with the first-aid kit.
**Stuck?** No **Print (A5)** button? Only hosts, planners and family can print, because paper can't hide phone numbers. Helpers read the card on screen.
