# Assistant

**What it is:** A helper you can ask about your wedding, in plain words. It reads your guests, functions, tasks and budget, and answers. It never changes anything.
**Why you'd use it:** Mausi calls to ask what time the haldi is. You answer in a few seconds, without opening five screens.

**Add-on, BETA:** The assistant is an add-on. If you don't see the round button at the bottom of the screen, the add-on is off. A host can check it in **Settings**, then **Plan & add-ons**. Answers in languages other than English carry a **BETA** badge.

## How to ask a quick question
1. Tap the round button, **Ask the assistant**. It sits at the bottom of the screen.
2. Under **Tap to ask**, pick a card, like **Next function**, **Who's coming** or **Due this week**.
3. If it asks **Which function?**, tap the one you mean.
4. Read the answer.

**Tip:** The cards work even when typing is off, so they are the fastest way to ask.
**Stuck?** Money answers, like **Budget** or **Payments due**, are only for hosts, planners and family. Helpers and viewers don't get them.

## How to type your own question
1. Tap **Ask the assistant**.
2. Type in the box that says **Ask about your wedding…**.
3. Tap **Send**.
4. Read the answer. To look up a guest, pick **Find a guest** and type the name.

**Tip:** The sheet shows how many typed questions are left today, like "{left} of {limit} typed questions left today".
**Stuck?** It says **I can't answer that yet**? Try one of the cards. If typing is off, the sheet says **Offline, the cards still work**.

## How to choose the answer language
1. Tap **Ask the assistant**.
2. Find **Answer language** at the top.
3. Pick a language from the list.
4. Ask your question again.

**Tip:** Languages marked **BETA** are machine-translated. A native speaker has not checked them yet.
**Stuck?** Answers come in English? Pick the language again, then ask a new question.
