# Moments

**What it is:** A small card that shows up when something good happens in your wedding. It says what happened and gives you one next step.
**Why you'd use it:** The last RSVP for the sangeet comes in. Krama tells you, so you don't have to go and check.

You see at most one card a day. It shows on Home, in the family feed and on a function's page. Tap **Got it** to put it away.

## What the moment cards mean
1. **That's {name} done.** A function has finished. Tap **See how it went** to read its recap.
2. **{name} is fully paid.** Every booking for that function is paid. Tap **Open Money**. Only people who can see money get this card.
3. **Every reply for {name} is in.** All invited families have answered. Tap **See who's coming**.
4. **Everyone on the list has an invitation.** Nobody is left out. Tap **See who hasn't replied**.

**Tip:** Cards for replies and invitations show to hosts, planners and family. Easy-reading mode shows only the first and third.
**Stuck?** Missed a card? A card shows once, for about a week, and a new one comes the next day. Open **Home** to see what is waiting.
