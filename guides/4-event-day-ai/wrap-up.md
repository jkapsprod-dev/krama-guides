# Wrap-up

**What it is:** A short list of loose ends after the wedding, one step at a time. You can skip any step and come back to it.
**Why you'd use it:** The photographer still owes the album, two vendors are not paid in full, and Bua is waiting for her thank-you.

Wrap-up is for hosts and planners. It opens the day after your last function. Before that, the page says **Wrap-up opens after your last function** and shows the date. The steps for vendors and thank-yous show only when Money is on.

## How to settle every vendor
1. Open **Wrap-up** from the menu, or tap **Open Wrap-up** on Home.
2. Open **Settle vendors**.
3. Read each vendor's line: **Quoted**, **Paid** and **Left**.
4. Add a **Note (optional)**, like "paid in full, receipt with the caterer".
5. Tap **Mark settled**. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo**.

**Tip:** The step says **Every vendor is settled** when nothing is owed. If one is not paid, tap **Open Money**.
**Stuck?** The step says **No vendor bookings to settle**? Bookings you add under Money show up here.

## How to track deliverables
1. Open the **Deliverables** step.
2. Under **Add a deliverable**, tap an idea under **Common deliverables**, like **Wedding album**. Or type it under **What are you waiting for?**
3. Add a **Due date (optional)**.
4. Tap **Add as a task**.

**Tip:** Under **What your vendors say**, you can see what your vendors listed and if it was delivered.
**Stuck?** Where did it go? It is now a task. Tap **Open Tasks** to see it.

## How to see the thank-yous still to send
1. Open the **Thank-yous** step.
2. Read how many thank-yous are left.
3. Tap **See all** in **Shagun** to write them.

**Tip:** The step says **No thank-yous waiting** when every envelope and gift has been thanked.
**Stuck?** The step is missing? It shows only when Money is on for your wedding.

## How to rate your planner
1. Open the **Feedback for your planner** step.
2. Under **How was {name}?**, pick a rating from 1 to 5.
3. Add a few words under **Anything you would like them to know (optional)**.
4. Tap **Send feedback**.

**Tip:** It is private. Only your planner's business sees it. It is never shown to guests or in public. To change it, tap **Update feedback**. To remove it, tap **Withdraw**.
**Stuck?** The step is missing? Only the host sees it, and only when a planner is linked.

## How to share the wedding in numbers
1. Open the **Finish** step. The page says **All wrapped up**.
2. Look at **Your wedding in numbers**: functions, guests invited, said yes, families, came and vendors.
3. Tap **Share to the family feed**.
4. Check the card, then tap **Post it**.

**Tip:** The card uses your wedding's own colours. If you change your mind, tap **Not now**.
**Stuck?** No share button? You can share only while the wedding is open for changes.

## How to download, then delete
1. Open the **Download, then delete** step.
2. Read the line that says when your data is kept until.
3. Under **Download everything**, tap **Prepare export**. When it is ready, tap **Download**.
4. To delete sooner, a host types the wedding's name under **Delete sooner**, then taps **Delete permanently**.

**Tip:** Download first. The delete option shows up only after your copy is ready.
**Stuck?** No delete box? Only a host can delete the wedding. The delete cannot be undone.
