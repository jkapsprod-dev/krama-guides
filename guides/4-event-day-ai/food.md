# Food (menu and counts)

**What it is:** The menu for a function, and a count sheet for the caterer. It shows how many people are expected, by meal type, and how many have arrived.
**Why you'd use it:** The caterer needs the Jain plates and the kids' plates split out, and you need to guarantee a final number.

Everyone in the wedding can read it. It shows counts only, never money. Hosts, planners and family can change the menu.

## How to write the menu
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function. Open the **Food** tab, or tap **Menu and counts**.
2. Scroll to **Change the menu**.
3. Under **Dishes**, type the **Dish**. Pick its **Course** and **Diet**.
4. Tap **Add dish**.
5. Repeat for each dish. To remove one, tap **Delete {name}**.

**Tip:** Tick **Live counter** for dishes made fresh at a counter, like chaat or dosa.
**Stuck?** The page says the menu is frozen? Dishes and the note can't change now. You can still change the plates guaranteed.

## How to set the plates guaranteed
1. Open the **Food** tab.
2. Scroll to **Note and guarantee**.
3. Type the number under **Plates guaranteed** and pick **Guarantee by**.
4. Add a line for the caterer under **Note for the caterer**, if you like.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** The count sheet shows who is expected from the RSVPs, split by meal choice. Use it to pick your number.
**Stuck?** The count says **Nobody has said yes to this function yet**? Replies have not come in yet.

## How to send it to the caterer
1. Open the **Food** tab.
2. Tap **Print for the caterer** for a paper sheet.
3. Or tap **Download CSV** to send a spreadsheet.

**Tip:** On the day, **Arrived** fills in from check-in. The sheet refreshes by itself every 30 seconds.
**Stuck?** Arrived counts say **about**? Some families have only partly arrived, so the numbers per meal are estimates.
