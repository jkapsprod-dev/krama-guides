# Notes from a meeting

**What it is:** A page that turns your meeting notes into short minutes and a list of tasks. You check them, then save.
**Why you'd use it:** You spent an hour with the caterer, and now nobody remembers who agreed to call the tent walla.

**Add-on, BETA:** This comes with the assistant add-on. If it says minutes come with the add-on, a host can check **Plan & add-ons** in **Settings**. Hosts, planners and family can use it. You can make 5 meetings a day for one wedding.

## How to turn meeting notes into minutes and tasks
1. Open **Tasks**, then tap **From a meeting**.
2. Under **Meeting**, give it a name, like "Caterer call". Pick the **Date**.
3. Type or paste your **Notes**. Or tap the microphone and speak. Each part can be up to a minute.
4. Tap **Make minutes and tasks**.
5. Read the draft. Under **Minutes** you see what was discussed and agreed. Under **Tasks** you see the jobs found.
6. Change anything. Untick a task you don't want. Pick **Who does it**, **Due** and **Function** if needed.
7. Tap **Save minutes and tasks**.

**Tip:** Say who does what and by when, like "Dhruv will confirm the tent by Friday". The draft gets better.
**Stuck?** It says it couldn't find minutes or tasks? Add a little more detail. Your notes stay on the page.
