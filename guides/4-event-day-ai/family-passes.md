# Family passes

**What it is:** One QR pass for every family, for the door to scan. Guests also find their own pass on their RSVP page.
**Why you'd use it:** Nani and Nana don't use a smartphone. You print their pass, and they walk in without any fuss.

## How to print family passes
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Family passes**. You can also open **Check-in** and tap **Print family passes**.
3. Look through the list. There is one pass per family.
4. Tap **Print passes**.

**Tip:** Print only for families without a smartphone. Everyone else can show the pass on their RSVP page.
**Stuck?** The list says **No families yet**? Add your guests first. Passes are made from the guest list.

## How to cancel a lost pass
1. Open **Family passes**.
2. Find the family.
3. Tap **New pass**.
4. Confirm when the app asks. The old pass stops working at every door.

**Tip:** Print the new pass again, so the family has one that works.
**Stuck?** Only hosts, planners and family can open this page. Helpers will not see it.
