# Function recap

**What it is:** A short summary of how one function went. How many came, how the run sheet went, and whether the key moment was on time.
**Why you'd use it:** The mehndi is over, and your cousin asks how many really came. You look it up and tell her.

## How to see how a function went
1. After the function, look on Home, the family feed or the function's page for a card that says **That's {name} done.**
2. Tap **See how it went**.
3. Read **Came**, **Said yes**, **Said no** and **No reply**.
4. Read **Run sheet**. It shows how many items were ticked, and the time of the last one.
5. Read the key moment line. It says **On time**, or how many minutes late or early.
6. At the bottom, tap the next function to keep going.

**Tip:** The recap only knows what you recorded. Tick the run sheet and use check-in on the day, and it fills in more.
**Stuck?** Can't find the card? It sits on Home, the family feed and the function's page, and goes away after you tap **Got it**. If the page says **No replies, arrivals or run-sheet ticks were recorded for this one**, nothing was recorded for that function.
