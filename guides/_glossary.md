# Glossary · one word for one thing · [[README]] · [[_inventory]]

Every guide note, caption and video uses these words. They match the app's own copy rules (`app\design\COPY.md`). When the app shows a label, the label wins: copy it exactly, in **bold**, even if it uses a word from the "Never" column (for example the chip **Attending**). The rules below are for our own sentences.

"Voice says" lines are Hinglish. They keep every button and screen name in English, exactly as the app shows it. The voice may say "plus button" for (+).

## People and roles

| Use | Never | Meaning and notes |
|---|---|---|
| **host** | owner, admin (in the wedding app), organiser | The role that runs the wedding. Usually the couple and their parents. Only a host approves quotes, plans and planners. |
| the couple | bride-and-groom team | The two people getting married. Use it for the reader in couple-side notes ("For the couple"). On screen their role is **Host**. |
| **family** (role) | relatives with access, editors | A member who can edit and see money, but can't manage members or settings. |
| a family (guests) | household, party, group, unit | Guests who share one RSVP link, like "the Mehta family". |
| **helper** | volunteer, staff (in the wedding app) | A member who sees only their tasks, the guests they're given and doors. Never sees money. Not the same as a "smart helper". |
| **viewer** | read-only user | A member who reads the wedding. Can react, report and add photos on the feed and gallery, but can't edit. |
| member | user, collaborator | Anyone with access to the wedding. **Members** is also the page name. |
| guest | invitee, attendee, visitor | Someone invited to a function. Guests never need an account. |
| **planner** | wedding planner company, agency | A planning business linked to a wedding. In the wedding it sees everything, including money. |
| **coordinator** | junior planner | Someone from the planner's team who helps on the day and never sees money (see [[planning-team]]). |
| **vendor** | supplier, service provider, partner | A business that does one job: decor, catering, photos. In the family's **Money** it's their list of **Vendors**. Never use marketplace or "find a vendor" words. |
| supplier | | Only on a business's **Purchases**, where it's the app's own label (**Supplier**). |
| client | customer | The family a planner or vendor works for, in business notes. |
| **Owner**, **Admin**, **Staff** | manager, employee | The three roles in a business's **Team**. |
| crew | workers, labour | People a business sends to a function. They need no account. |
| driver | | A person on a pickup run. Opens their own link; no account. |
| Krama support | the system, Krama team, admin | Krama's own people, for example in **Krama support access**. |

## Writing rules that go with these words

- Grade 8 or lower, short sentences, one action per step (PROMPTS.md Appendix C).
- No exclamation marks, no "Oops", no em or en dashes in our text. The only dash allowed is inside an exact app label, like **Has money details — hide from helpers and viewers** (Jay, 2C).
- Made-up names only: the Sharma-Patel wedding, Riya Sharma (host), Kavya Sharma (helper), Neha Desai (Utsav Planners), Imran Shaikh (Rangoli Decor), and the families the notes use (Mehta family, Meena Bua, Mama ji and others). Never real people.
