# Getting around

**What it is:** The bottom bar, the (+) button, **More** and the search box get you to any part of your wedding in a tap or two.
**Why you'd use it:** Mausi calls to ask when the haldi starts. You are in the car, so you search "haldi" and read her the time.

## How to add anything from the (+)
1. Look at the bar at the bottom of your phone. Tap the round (+) button in the middle.
2. The **Add to your wedding** sheet opens.
3. Tap what you want to add, like **Add a guest**, **Add a task** or **Add an expense**.
4. Fill in the form and save it.

**Tip:** Under **Add a guest**, tap **From a list** to add many guests at once. In the week of the wedding, **Add a photo** moves to the top.
**Stuck?** No (+) on your bar? Viewers don't have one, and nobody can add while a wedding is read-only. Helpers see only **Add a photo**, **Post to the family feed** and **Remind me**.

## How to find a guest, function or task fast
1. Open **Search the wedding**. On a phone, tap the search icon at the top. On a computer, press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) or click the search box.
2. Before you type, tap a chip under **Quick**, like **Due this week**. Or pick something under **Recent**.
3. Or type a name, or part of one, like "haldi" or "Mausi".
4. Tap the result. It opens that guest, function, task, member or file.

**Tip:** Look under **Actions** in the results. You can jump straight to **Add a guest** from there.
**Stuck?** If it says "Nothing found", try a shorter word. You only find what your role may see. Helpers find their own tasks and the families they are given.

## How to find a section on your phone
1. Tap **More** at the right end of the bottom bar.
2. Find your section in one of five groups: **Plan**, **People**, **Money**, **On the day** or **Keepsakes**.
3. Tap its tile.
4. For your own settings, scroll down to **Notifications**, **Personalise** and **Codes & planner**. Hosts and planners also see **Settings**.
5. At the very bottom you find **Switch wedding**, **Your account**, **Sign out** and **Leave this wedding**.

**Tip:** **Journey** and **Wedding Sheet** sit under **Plan**. Your bottom bar depends on your role. Hosts, planners and family see **Home**, **Guests**, (+), **Tasks** and **More**. Helpers see **Home**, **Tasks**, (+), **Event day** and **More**. Viewers see **Home**, **Functions**, **Guests** and **More**.
**Stuck?** A tile is missing? Your role may not use that section. Helpers and viewers never see **Money**. **Wrap-up** shows greyed out until the day after your last function.

## How to collapse the sidebar on a computer
1. On a computer, look at the sidebar on the left.
2. At the bottom of the sidebar, click **Collapse the sidebar**.
3. The sidebar shrinks to a narrow strip of icons. Hold your mouse over an icon to see its name.
4. To bring the names back, click **Expand the sidebar**.

**Tip:** Krama remembers your choice. You can also set it in **Personalise**, under **Sidebar**: **Full** or **Icon rail**.
**Stuck?** You can't see a sidebar? On a phone or a narrow window there isn't one. Use the bottom bar and **More** instead.
