# Home

**What it is:** Home shows what needs you today: the countdown, the replies that came in, and what changed while you were away.
**Why you'd use it:** On Monday morning you open Krama on the train. Three families replied over the weekend, and Home tells you before you ask.

## How to read your Home
1. Open **Home**.
2. Read the wedding card at the top. It shows your names, the dates and the countdown. Its main button changes with the day. On a function's day it says **Open event day**.
3. Find **Wedding readiness**. It says "Set-up 3 of 8" and shows what comes **Next:**.
4. Read **Since you last looked**. It lists replies, payments and new members. Tap **Got it** when you have seen them.
5. Check **RSVPs by function**. It shows who is coming, who can't come and who is waiting, for each function.
6. Scroll down to **Recent activity** to see who changed what.

**Tip:** Under **RSVPs by function**, tap **Send reminders**. Krama opens your own app with a ready message, and you press send.
**Stuck?** You can't see **Wedding readiness** or money cards? Those are only for hosts, planners and family. Helpers and viewers see a Home made for their role.

## How to arrange your Home
1. Scroll to the buttons under the cards on **Home**.
2. Tap **Arrange**.
3. Drag a card to a new place. Or use the up and down arrows on the card.
4. Tap **Wider** or **Narrower** to change the width of a card.
5. Tap **Hide** to take a card off Home. On a phone, **Hide** is an icon.
6. Tap **Done**.

**Tip:** Only your own Home changes. Nobody else sees your layout. The wedding card always stays, but you can move it.
**Stuck?** If it says "Your layout could not be saved", tap **Done** and try again. **Arrange** only shows when you have more than one card.

## How to add a widget
1. Scroll down on **Home**.
2. Tap **Add widget**.
3. Look at the list. Each widget has a name and a short note.
4. Tap **Add** beside the widget you want.
5. The list closes and the card is on your Home.

**Tip:** A card you hid comes back the same way.
**Stuck?** No **Add widget** button? Then everything you may have is already on your Home. Money cards only show for people who can see money.
