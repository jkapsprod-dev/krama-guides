# Personalise

**What it is:** The page where you choose how Krama looks and feels on your own phone or computer.
**Why you'd use it:** Nani finds the small text hard to read on the family phone, so you make everything bigger for her in a minute.

Each section is marked **You** (only you see it) or **Everyone** (the whole wedding sees it). Only the wedding theme is **Everyone**.

On a phone, tap **More**, then **Personalise**. On a computer, hosts and planners open **Settings** and pick **Personalise**. Family, helpers and viewers find **Personalise** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## How to switch to light or dark
1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Light or dark**.
3. Tap **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** **Auto** follows your phone, so the app turns dark by itself in the evening.
**Stuck?** **Save** stays grey until you change something. Pick an option first.

## How to pick a look
1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Look**. It is the first section.
3. Tap a look: **Patrika**, **Bahi**, **Chitra**, **Mehfil**, **Jharokha** or **Sutra**. Each tile shows your own wedding.
4. On a phone, tap **Preview** to see it before you save.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** A look changes the layout and feel. The wedding theme sets the colours. You can use any look with any theme.
**Stuck?** If your Home did not change, you arranged your own Home before. Tap the button that starts with **Use** and ends with **Home order** to take the look's order.

## How to change the wedding theme
Only a host can do this. It changes the colours for every member and every guest card.

1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Wedding theme**.
3. Tap a theme: **Haveli**, **Lipi**, **Zari**, **Neel**, **Kesar**, **Mogra**, **Shyam** or **Chandni**.
4. Read the question. It tells you how many members will see the change.
5. Tap **Change theme**. You see "Theme changed." You do not need to press **Save**.

**Tip:** Changed your mind? Tap **Keep the current theme**. Everyone keeps their own light or dark mode, text size and spacing.
**Stuck?** The tiles are grey if you are not a host. The page says who to ask. They are also grey when the wedding is read-only.

## How to make text bigger or use easy reading
1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Text size**.
3. Tap **Standard**, **Larger** or **Easy reading**.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Easy reading** makes everything big and turns off animations. Home becomes simpler, with **Read aloud** and **Call {name}** buttons. The bottom bar always stays on.

**Tip:** Use **Spacing** (**Comfortable** or **Compact**) to fit more or less on the screen. Buttons stay easy to tap.
**Stuck?** To switch easy reading off, scroll to the bottom of Home and tap **Personalise**. **Read aloud** works in English only for now.

## How to choose what is on your Home
Only your own Home changes.

1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Home widgets**.
3. Under **On your Home**, tap the up or down arrow to move a widget.
4. Tap **Wide** to make a widget full width. Tap the X to remove it.
5. Under **Not on your Home**, tap **Add** beside a widget you want.
6. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Tap **Reset to wedding defaults** to start again.
**Stuck?** The **Wedding card** always stays on Home. Money widgets show only for people who can see money.

## How to set the bottom bar, sidebar and start screen
1. Open **Personalise**.
2. Find **Bottom bar**. Tap **Always show** or **Hide while scrolling**.
3. Find **Sidebar**. Tap **Full** or **Icon rail**.
4. Find **Open the app on**. Tap **Home** or **Journey**.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** **Bottom bar** is for phones. **Sidebar** is for computers. **Icon rail** gives the page more room.
**Stuck?** You do not see **Bottom bar** in easy reading, because the bar is always on there.
