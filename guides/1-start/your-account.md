# Your account

**What it is:** Your account page is where you manage your password and see where you are signed in.
**Why you'd use it:** Your Mama ji used your phone to check the sangeet list. Before you hand it back, you want to sign him out of your account.

To open it, tap **Your account** under **More** in a wedding. You also find it at the bottom of **Your weddings**.

## How to set, change or remove your password
1. Open **Your account**.
2. Under **Password**, type a **New password**. Use at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number.
3. If Krama asks for your **Current password**, type it first.
4. Tap **Set password**. If you already have one, the button says **Change password**.
5. To remove it, tap **Remove password**, then tap **Remove password** again to confirm. You sign in with an emailed link from then on.

**Tip:** Changing or removing a password signs out your other devices. Krama also emails you when a password is removed.
**Stuck?** If the page says "For your safety, setting a password needs a recent sign-in", tap **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the link, and you come back here. If it says "Your password is your only way in", you can't remove it yet.

## How to see your signed-in devices
1. Open **Your account**.
2. Scroll to **Signed-in devices**.
3. Read each line. It shows the device and "Last active" with a date and time.
4. Your own phone says **This device**.

**Tip:** A device you don't know, such as a computer you never used? Sign out everywhere else, as shown next.
**Stuck?** A phone can show as **Unknown device**. Check the "Last active" time to work out whose it is.

## How to sign out other devices
1. Open **Your account**.
2. Go to **Signed-in devices**.
3. Tap **Sign out everywhere else**.
4. The other devices are signed out. Your phone stays signed in.

**Tip:** Do this after you lend your phone, or after you use a shared computer.
**Stuck?** You don't see **Sign out everywhere else**? Then no other device is signed in.
