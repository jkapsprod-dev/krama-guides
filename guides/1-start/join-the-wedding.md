# Join the wedding

**What it is:** Open the invite a host sent you, and you become part of their wedding in Krama.
**Why you'd use it:** Riya's mother sends you an invite on WhatsApp. You are her sister, and you want to see the haldi plan.

## How to accept an email invite
1. Open the invite link the host sent you. It opens the Krama sign-up page with your email filled in.
2. Tap **Create an account**. Type **Your name** and a **Password**, then tap **Create account**. Already have an account? Tap **Sign in** instead.
3. Use the same email the invite was sent to.
4. If you made a new account, open the link in the email we send you.
5. You land in the wedding. Krama adds you when you sign in.

**Tip:** Your role is set by the host. A helper sees only their own tasks and guests, and never the money.
**Stuck?** You signed in with a different email? Then the invite does not match. Ask the host to invite the email you use.

## How to accept a WhatsApp invite
1. Open the link in the WhatsApp message. You see **Join the wedding**.
2. Not signed in yet? Tap **Create an account** or **Sign in**. Then you come back to this page.
3. Type the **WhatsApp number** the invite was sent to. Indian numbers need no +91.
4. Tap **Join**.
5. You land in the wedding.

**Tip:** The link is for you alone. Don't forward it.
**Stuck?** "This link is locked" means you typed the wrong number too many times. "This link ran out" means it is more than 14 days old. "This link has been used" means someone already joined with it. In each case, ask the host to tap **New link** in Members, and they send you a fresh one.
