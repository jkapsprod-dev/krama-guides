# Leave a wedding or delete my account

**What it is:** Two ways to step away. You can leave one wedding, or you can delete your whole Krama account.
**Why you'd use it:** Your cousin finished helping with the sangeet and wants out of the notifications, or you no longer want an account at all.

Leaving a wedding takes you out of that one wedding. Deleting your account removes you from Krama for good.

## How to leave a wedding
1. Tap **More**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Leave this wedding**.
3. Read the message. You lose access straight away, and someone must invite you again to come back.
4. Tap **Yes, leave**. You land on **Your weddings**.

**Tip:** On a computer, **Leave this wedding** is at the bottom of the **Members** page.
**Stuck?** If it says "A wedding always needs at least one host. Make someone else a host first.", you are the last host. See "How to hand things over first" below.

## How to delete my account
This cannot be undone.

1. Tap **More**, then **Your account**.
2. Scroll to **Delete my account**.
3. Read **What we delete** and **What stays**.
4. If it asks, tap **Sign in again**. Then come back to this page.
5. Type DELETE in capital letters.
6. Tap **Delete my account**. You see "Your account is deleted" and get a short email.

**Tip:** What you added to a wedding stays there. It shows you as a former member.
**Stuck?** The button stays grey until you type DELETE exactly, in capital letters. If a box called **Hand these over first** shows instead, follow the next steps.

## How to hand things over first
You only see this if you lead something on your own, like the only host of a wedding.

1. On **Your account**, find **Hand these over first**.
2. Tap **Open members** beside the wedding.
3. Find a family member. Use **Change role for** their name and pick **Host**.
4. Go back to **Your account**. The **Delete my account** form is now there.

**Tip:** You can also tap **Open settings** and delete the wedding. For a business, tap **Open team** and make a teammate an owner.
**Stuck?** Only a host can make someone else a host. A business with no other people cannot be closed in the app yet.
