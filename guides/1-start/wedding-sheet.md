# Wedding Sheet

**What it is:** The Wedding Sheet is the whole wedding on one page: overview, functions, guests, money, stays and the decisions made.
**Why you'd use it:** Papa wants the whole plan on one page for the family meeting, and Bua in Pune wants to read it without signing up.

## How to see the whole wedding on one page
1. Open **Home**.
2. Tap **Sheet** in the switch at the top.
3. Read **Overview**. It shows the dates, the number of functions and the reply-by date.
4. Use **On this page** to jump to **Functions**, **Guests**, **Money**, **Stays** or **Decisions**.
5. To leave a note for the family, type in **Introduction**. Anyone who can edit may do this.

**Tip:** You can also open it from **More**, under **Plan**. Each function has a **Line for the Sheet**. **Decisions** fills itself when the plan changes. Nobody types it.
**Stuck?** You can't type in **Introduction**? Your role may be read only, or the wedding may be read only. You only see **Money** if you are a host, planner or family member.

## How to print it
1. Open **Wedding Sheet**.
2. Tap **Print or save as PDF**.
3. In the print box, pick your printer. Or pick the choice to save as a PDF.
4. Print or save.

**Tip:** Each section starts on a new page. Money is left out of the printed Sheet.
**Stuck?** Missing a Money page? That is on purpose. The printed Sheet never has money.

## How to share a read-only link
1. Open **Wedding Sheet**.
2. Tap **Share read-only link**.
3. Tap **Create link**.
4. Tap **Copy link**.
5. Paste the link into your own WhatsApp or email, and press send. Krama sends nothing by itself.

**Tip:** Anyone with the link can read the page without signing in. It shows no money, and no guest names or phone numbers. To stop sharing, open **Share read-only link** and tap **Switch off**. To replace the link, tap **New link** and confirm. The old link stops at once.
**Stuck?** Only hosts and planners see **Share read-only link**. It says "Sharing is paused while the wedding is read-only"? Then you can copy the link but not change it.
