# Language

**What it is:** A list where you pick the language you read Krama in.
**Why you'd use it:** Nani reads Hindi much faster than English, so you switch her phone to Hindi and she can follow the wedding plan herself.

Krama has English and 8 other languages: Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Marwari, Konkani and Punjabi. English has been checked by native speakers. The other 8 show **BETA**, which means native speakers are still checking them.

## How to change the app's language
1. Tap **More**, then **Personalise**. On a computer, open **Personalise** from the sidebar or from **Settings**.
2. Find **Language**.
3. Tap the list and pick a language. Each name is written in its own script.
4. Look for **BETA** next to the name. A note explains that some wording may be off.
5. That is all. There is no **Save** button, because the language saves as soon as you pick it.

**Tip:** Your choice follows you to your other phones and computers. Guests can pick their own language on their RSVP page too.
**Stuck?** Some words may still show in English. That is normal for a **BETA** language. If you see "Couldn't change the language. Please try again.", pick it once more.
