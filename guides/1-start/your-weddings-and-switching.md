# Your weddings and switching

**What it is:** **Your weddings** lists every wedding you are part of, and the switcher moves you between weddings and businesses.
**Why you'd use it:** Neha plans two weddings this month. Today she must jump from the Sharma-Patel mehndi to another family's sangeet, with one phone and one sign-in.

## How to open another wedding
1. On a phone, tap **More**.
2. Tap **Switch wedding**.
3. On **Your weddings**, find the wedding you want. Each wedding has its own card.
4. Tap the card. That wedding's Home opens.

**Tip:** On a computer, click the wedding name at the top of the sidebar to reach **Your weddings**. Each card shows the next function, your open tasks and how long the data is kept.
**Stuck?** If you have one wedding and no business, Krama skips the list and opens that wedding. Tap **More**, then **Switch wedding**, to see the list. To add another wedding, tap **Start a new wedding** on this page.

## How to switch between a wedding and a business
1. Tap the wedding name at the top of your screen.
2. The **Switch wedding or account** sheet opens.
3. Look under **Weddings** and **Businesses**. The one you are in is in bold.
4. Tap the one you want. The sheet closes and that wedding or business opens.

**Tip:** On a computer, click **Switch account** near the top of the sidebar instead.
**Stuck?** The switcher only shows when you have a wedding and a business, or two businesses. If you only have weddings, use **Your weddings** as shown above.

## How to find a wedding with the filters
1. Open **Your weddings**.
2. Find the chips under the title, in the row called **Show weddings**.
3. Tap **Upcoming** to see weddings that still have a function ahead.
4. Tap **This month** to see weddings whose next function is this month.
5. Tap **Needs a decision** to see weddings that will be deleted soon or are read-only.
6. Tap **All** to see every wedding again.

**Tip:** The number on each chip tells you how many weddings are inside it.
**Stuck?** The chips only show when you have two or more weddings. With one wedding there is nothing to filter.
