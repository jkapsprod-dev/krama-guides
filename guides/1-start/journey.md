# Journey

**What it is:** Journey shows your wedding week by week, from this week to the wedding week, with each function, task and payment under the week it falls in.
**Why you'd use it:** Seven weeks to go, and Papa asks what happens when. You show him the tailor's last payment, the invitations and the first arrivals, week by week.

## How to see the wedding week by week
1. Open **Home**.
2. Tap **Journey** in the switch at the top. The switch has **Dashboard**, **Journey** and **Sheet**.
3. Read the title. It tells you how many weeks are left to the wedding week.
4. Look down the lanes: **Functions**, **Tasks**, **Payments**, **Guests & RSVPs** and **Arrivals & stays**. Each item sits under the week it falls in.
5. On a phone, tap the arrow buttons for **Next week** and **Previous week**. On a computer, scroll sideways.
6. Tap an item's name to open it.

**Tip:** You can also reach it from **More**, under **Plan**. The week you are in is marked **This week**. Overdue items wait under **Earlier**, and items with no date wait under **No date yet**.
**Stuck?** If it says "No dates yet", give a function a date. Tap **Go to Functions** to do it. The **Payments** lane only shows for hosts, planners and family.

## How to move something to another week
1. Find a task or a payment on **Journey**. Functions can't be moved here.
2. Tap **Move** under its name.
3. Tap **Earlier week** or **Later week** to shift it by one week.
4. Or choose a day under **Date**, then tap **Set date**.
5. The card jumps to its new week. Its due date has changed.

**Tip:** On a computer, you can also drag the card to another week. Every move is written on the **Decisions** list of the [[wedding-sheet]].
**Stuck?** "Someone else changed this item, so it wasn't moved" means another person edited it first. The page now shows the latest, so try again. "You can't move items in this wedding" means your role is read only, or the wedding is read only.
