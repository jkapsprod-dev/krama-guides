# Set up your wedding

**What it is:** Answer a few questions, and Krama sets up your functions, checklist and budget lines for you.
**Why you'd use it:** The engagement is done, the families are calling, and nothing is written down. In ten minutes you have a plan to share.

You answer nine questions, then check a summary. You can change every answer later.

## How to start a new wedding
1. Open **Your weddings**. Tap **Start a new wedding**.
2. On **Who are you?**, pick one. For example, **One of the couple** or **A parent or family member**. Tap **Next**.
3. On **What should we call this wedding?**, type the **Wedding name**, like "Riya & Arjun".
4. Tap **Next**.

**Tip:** On a computer, your wedding card on the right fills in as you answer.
**Stuck?** "Please give the wedding a name" means the name is empty. Type one and tap **Next** again.

## How to choose traditions and functions
1. On **Which tradition(s)?**, tick every tradition that applies. Mixed weddings are fine.
2. Tap **Next**.
3. On **Which functions are you having?**, the usual ones are already ticked. Untick any you are skipping.
4. To add your own, type a name under **Add another function** and tap **Add**. You can add up to 10.
5. Tap **Next**.

**Tip:** With two traditions, functions they share show once, under **Shared by your traditions**.
**Stuck?** Customs differ from family to family. Check the suggested functions with your elders.

## How to add the date, guests and budget
1. On **When and where?**, choose **I know the date**, **I know the month** or **Not decided yet**. Then pick the day or month.
2. Type the **City**. If most guests will travel and stay, tick the destination wedding box. Tap **Next**.
3. On **Roughly how many guests?**, pick a range, such as **Fewer than 100**. Tap **Next**.
4. On **Roughly what's the budget?**, pick a range, or pick **Prefer not to say**.
5. To share costs, tick **Split the shared costs between the two families** and type **Bride's family pays (%)**.
6. Tap **Next**.

**Tip:** The budget is private. Only hosts, planners and family can see it.
**Stuck?** Don't know the date yet? Pick **Not decided yet**. You can set it later in Functions.

## How to add helpers, choose how long to keep your data, and create your wedding
1. On **Who's helping?**, tap **Add a person**. Type an **Email address** and pick a **Role**. This is optional, so tap **Next** to skip it.
2. On **How long should we keep your data?**, choose **A number of days after the last function**, or **On a specific date**. Most families choose 60 days. Tap **Next**.
3. On **Ready to go?**, check the summary. Tap **Change** next to anything you want to fix.
4. If a planner or a friend gave you a code, type it under **Codes (optional)**. Use **Planner code** or **A friend's referral code**.
5. Tap **Create my wedding**.
6. Your wedding opens, with your functions and checklist ready.

**Tip:** A planner code links that planner to your wedding at once, so they can see and edit it. A friend's code shows them nothing about your wedding.
**Stuck?** "Nothing was created" means something went wrong. Your answers are safe on the screen, so tap **Create my wedding** again. If it says new weddings are paused, try again later.
