# Run sheets

**What it is:** A run sheet is the minute-by-minute plan for one function: what happens, when, where, and who is in charge.
**Why you'd use it:** The band, the pandit and the welcome desk all ask "when do we start?" You put the times on one sheet, and everyone reads the same plan.

Hosts, planners and family can add and change items. Helpers and viewers can read the sheet and tick items on the day.

## How to build a run sheet
1. Open **Run sheets**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Choose the function from the chips at the top.
3. Tap **Add item**.
4. Set the **Time** and type **What happens**, like "Baraat leaves the gate".
5. Choose **Who's in charge** and, if it helps, a **Vendor**.
6. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Fill in **Where** too, so the sheet shows the place for each item. For something after midnight, pick the next day in **Date**.
**Stuck?** If the sheet says the function has no date, items have no times. Set the date in the function first.

## How to set the muhurat and see clashes
1. Open **Functions** and tap the function.
2. Tap **Rituals**.
3. Under **Muhurat**, pick the **Date** and the **Time** your pandit, qazi or granthi gave.
4. Tap **Save muhurat**.
5. Open **Run sheets** and choose the same function.
6. Look for the muhurat line at the top. Items marked **Close to the muhurat** are within 15 minutes of it.

**Tip:** Move the marked items if you can. Then the line says nothing else is planned near the muhurat.
**Stuck?** Don't see the muhurat line? The muhurat is not set yet. Look for **Set the muhurat** on the function page and tap **Set it**.

## How to print or share a run sheet
1. Open **Run sheets** and choose the function.
2. Tap **Print run sheet**.
3. Tap **Print** on the page that opens, or save it as a PDF.
4. To share it, go back and tap **Share read-only**. The link is copied.
5. Paste the link in your family group.

**Tip:** Print a copy for the pandit and the decorator. Paper still works when the phone is busy.
**Stuck?** The read-only link works only for people who are members of your wedding. Others must sign in. For everyone else, print the sheet.
