# Tasks

**What it is:** Tasks is your wedding to-do list. Each task has an owner, a due date and sometimes a function.
**Why you'd use it:** Your bua says she will book the mehendi artist, and then nobody hears back. You give her the task with a date, and the whole family can see if it is done.

Hosts, planners and family can add and change tasks. A helper sees only the tasks given to them and can tick them off. A viewer can read.

## How to add a task and give it to someone
1. Tap the (+) button, then **Add a task**. On a computer, open **Tasks** and click **New task**.
2. Type **What needs doing?**, like "Book the mehendi artist".
3. Choose a person under **Assign to**.
4. Pick a **Due date**.
5. Tap **Save task**.

**Tip:** Choose a **Function** too, so the task also shows on that function's page. Add a **Checklist** with one item per line for small steps.
**Stuck?** If you can't find the person under **Assign to**, invite them first in **Members**.

## How to tick a task done
1. Open **Tasks**. The bottom bar on a phone has it.
2. Find the task. Overdue tasks are at the top.
3. Tap the box beside the task.
4. The task moves down to **Done**. Tap the box again to undo it.

**Tip:** A helper can tick their own tasks too. That way you know without asking.
**Stuck?** Viewers can't tick tasks. A helper can tick only the tasks given to them.

## How to use the board
1. Open **Tasks** on a computer.
2. Click **Board** next to **List**.
3. Find the columns: **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**.
4. Drag a task card to another column. Or use the stage list on the card.

**Tip:** Use the function chips at the top to show the board for one function only, like Haldi.
**Stuck?** The **Board** switch shows only on a bigger screen. On a phone, the list works the same way.

## How to see only your tasks
1. Open **Tasks**.
2. Choose **Mine** under **Show tasks**.
3. To see all tasks again, choose **Everyone**.

**Tip:** Tap **More filters** to look at one person's tasks, or one category such as **Vendors**, then tap **Apply**.
**Stuck?** Helpers don't see **Mine** and **Everyone**. They always see only their own tasks.
