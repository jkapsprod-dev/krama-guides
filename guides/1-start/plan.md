# Plan

**What it is:** Plan is the family's decision room. It holds approvals, decisions, the brief, changes, trials and freezes, so nothing depends on memory.
**Why you'd use it:** Your planner shows two haldi decor designs. The host says "peach", and three weeks later nobody remembers who said it. In Plan, it is written down with a name and a date.

On a phone, tap **More**, then **Plan**. Helpers can't open Plan. Viewers can read **Decisions**, **Brief**, **Trials** and **Freezes**. **Approvals** and **Changes** are for hosts, planners and family, because they involve money.

## How to ask the family to approve something
1. Open **Plan**, then **Approvals**.
2. Under **Ask the family to approve something**, type **What needs approving**, like "Peach haldi décor".
3. Choose the **Kind**, like **Design** or **Menu**. Add **Needed by** if there is a deadline.
4. Tap **Ask for approval**.
5. A host opens it and taps **Approve**. If something must change, the host taps **Ask for changes** and writes **What should change?**

**Tip:** Tap **Remind the family** on a waiting request. Krama allows one reminder a day.
**Stuck?** Only a host can approve. A planner or family member sees "A host approves this."

## How to note a decision
1. Open **Plan**, then **Decisions**.
2. Type **What was decided**, like "Peach for haldi".
3. Type **Decided by**, like "Groom's mother".
4. Pick the **Date**. Pick a **Function**, or leave it as **Whole wedding**.
5. Tap **Add to log**.

**Tip:** Add decisions the same day. A short line is enough.
**Stuck?** Viewers can read the log but can't add to it. Hosts and planners can remove an entry.

## How to fill in the brief
1. Open **Plan**, then **Brief**.
2. Answer the questions, like **Style and mood**, **Colours** and **Must-haves**.
3. Fill in **Dietary rules**, **Elders and access needs** and **Venue rules** too.
4. Tap **Save brief**.

**Tip:** Short answers are fine. They stay in your wedding and are deleted with it.
**Stuck?** Only hosts and planners can edit the brief. Everyone else reads it.

## How to book a trial or tasting
1. Open **Plan**, then **Trials**.
2. Choose the **Kind**, like **Menu tasting**. Type **What it is**.
3. Pick the **Date** and **Time**. Type **Where**.
4. Tap **Schedule it**.
5. After the trial, tap **Mark done**.
6. Write **How did it go?** and tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Choose **With which vendor?** and they see the title, time and place on their calendar. They never see who is going.
**Stuck?** To cancel a trial, tap **Cancel it**. The vendor sees that it was cancelled.

## How to freeze the guest list or menu
1. Open **Plan**, then **Freezes**.
2. Find what to freeze: **Guest list**, **Rooming list**, **Menu** or **Final headcount**.
3. Pick the **Freeze on** date. For **Final headcount**, also type the **Promised headcount**.
4. Tap **Set the date**.
5. To allow changes again, type **Why lift it?** and tap **Lift the freeze**.

**Tip:** From the freeze date, the family and planner can't change that part. Guests can still reply and fix their own details.
**Stuck?** Only hosts and planners can set or lift a freeze. The reason you give goes into the decision log.

## How to send a change for approval
1. Open **Plan**, then **Changes**.
2. Type **What is changing**, like "200 more guests at the reception". Describe it under **What changes**.
3. Choose **Costs more** or **Saves money**, and type the **Amount (₹)**.
4. Tap **Save draft**.
5. Tap **Send for approval** on the draft, then confirm.
6. A host decides under **Approvals**.

**Tip:** Pick a vendor under **Tell this vendor**. The description goes into their brief, so don't put prices in it.
**Stuck?** You can't edit a change after you send it. Remove it only while it is still a draft.
