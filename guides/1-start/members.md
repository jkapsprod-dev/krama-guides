# Members

**What it is:** Members is the list of people who help plan your wedding. You choose what each person can see and do.
**Why you'd use it:** Your cousin Kavya offers to help with the guest list and the pickups. You invite her as a helper. She sees only her own tasks and guests, and never the money.

Only hosts and planners can open **Members**. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Members**.

## How to invite family
1. Open **Members**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, type **Their name**. Add an **Email**, a **WhatsApp number**, or both.
3. Choose a **Role**. Choose a **Side** if it helps.
4. Tap **Send invite**.
5. When **Invite ready** shows, tap **Send by email** or **Send on WhatsApp**. Your own email or WhatsApp opens with the message ready.
6. You press send. Krama sends nothing by itself.

**Tip:** To bring in your planner, choose the role **Planner**. Each role shows a short line that says what it can do.
**Stuck?** Invites last 14 days. If an email doesn't arrive, send the link on WhatsApp instead. If **Send invite** says the email already has an invite, find it under **Pending invites**.

## How to change someone's role or remove them
1. Open **Members**.
2. Find the person in the list.
3. Use **Change role for** their name, and pick the new role.
4. To remove them, tap the bin icon beside their name.
5. Tap **Yes, remove**. They lose access straight away.

**Tip:** The table **What each role can do** on the same page shows who can see what, from **Host** to **Viewer**.
**Stuck?** A wedding always needs one host. Make someone else a host first. Only a host can change or remove another host.

## How to let a helper see some guests
1. Open **Members**.
2. Find the helper. A box called **Guest tags** with their name shows below it.
3. Type the tags, separated by commas, like "pune, college".
4. Tap **Save tags**.

**Tip:** A helper already sees the guests of the functions they are in charge of. Tags add more guests on top.
**Stuck?** The box shows only for helpers. For any other role, there is nothing to set.

## How to resend an expired invite
1. Open **Members**.
2. Find the person under **Pending invites**. It says **Invite expired**.
3. Tap **New link**.
4. Tap **Send on WhatsApp**.
5. You press send. The old link stops working.

**Tip:** Check **Pending invites** a few days after you invite people. It shows how many days are left.
**Stuck?** **New link** works for invites sent to a WhatsApp number. For an email invite, remove it with the bin icon and invite them again.
