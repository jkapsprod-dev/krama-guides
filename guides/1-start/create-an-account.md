# Create an account

**What it is:** Your Krama account is your name, your email and a password, so you can open your wedding on any phone.
**Why you'd use it:** Your cousin Riya invites you to help plan the sangeet. You need an account before you can see anything.

## How to create an account
1. Open the Krama sign-in page. Tap **Create an account**, under "New to Krama?".
2. Type **Your name** and your **Email address**.
3. Type a **Password**. Use at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number.
4. Tap **Create account**.
5. **Check your email** appears. Open the link in the email we sent. It can take a minute, so look in spam too.
6. You land in Krama. If you came for a wedding, you go there. If you are new, the set-up questions start.

**Tip:** If someone invited you to a wedding, use the same email address they invited. You then join their wedding on your own.
**Stuck?** No email after a few minutes? Tap **Send it again** on the **Check your email** screen. If Krama asks you to **Choose your password** again, you opened the link on a different phone. Type it once more and tap **Save password**.

## How to set a password after you confirm
You see this if you came in with an email link and have no password yet.
1. On the first set-up question, tap **Set a password (optional)**.
2. Type a **Password**. Use at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number.
3. Tap **Save password**.
4. You see "Password saved." Next time you sign in, you can use it.

**Tip:** Skip it any time and keep using email links. You can add a password later in [[your-account]].
**Stuck?** "Please use at least 10 characters" or "too easy to guess" means the password is too weak. Add more letters and numbers.
