# Data and privacy

**What it is:** The Settings page that shows how long Krama keeps your wedding, and lets you download it, extend it or delete it.
**Why you'd use it:** The wedding is over, the thank-you cards are done, and you want a copy of the guest list and bills before everything is deleted.

Only hosts and planners can open this page. Family, helpers and viewers cannot.

## How to see how long your data is kept
1. Tap **More**, then **Settings**.
2. Find the top card, **Your wedding's data is kept until**. It shows a date.
3. Read the line under the date. It says how the date is set.
4. Read **What happens when**. It lists the reminders, when the wedding becomes read-only, and when everything is deleted.

**Tip:** Krama reminds every host and planner 30 days, 7 days and 72 hours before. A banner with **Extend or export** also shows up.
**Stuck?** If the date moved, a function date changed. By default the date follows your last function.

## How to keep everything longer
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **How long to keep everything**, find **Keep until**.
3. Pick **After the last function** and type 7 to 365 days. Or pick **On a fixed date** and choose **Delete on**.
4. Read the line "Data will be kept until" and the date.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** A planner can make it longer, but not shorter than a host set.
**Stuck?** "Pick a date at least 72 hours from now." means the fixed date is too close. Pick a later one.

## How to download everything
1. Open **Settings**. Scroll to **Download everything**.
2. Tap **Prepare export**.
3. Wait. You get a note in **Notifications** when it is ready.
4. Tap **Download**.

**Tip:** You get one ZIP with every list as a spreadsheet, the guest list, the budget summary and your files. The link works for 7 days. Keep the file somewhere private.
**Stuck?** "You've made the most exports allowed today." means you reached today's limit. Your last export is still there to download.

## How to delete the wedding now
Only a host can do this. It cannot be undone.

1. Open **Settings**. Scroll to **Delete everything now**.
2. Read what goes: all functions, guests, money records and files.
3. Type the wedding name in the box, exactly as shown above it.
4. Tap **Delete permanently**. It only works after the name matches.

**Tip:** Download everything first. Krama keeps a deletion certificate with counts only and no personal details.
**Stuck?** "The name doesn't match. Type it exactly as shown." means a letter or space is off. Copy the name from the screen.

## How to answer a Krama support request
1. Open **Settings**. Find **Krama support access**.
2. If Krama support has asked, read which parts they want and for how many hours.
3. A host taps **Allow** or **Decline**. Support can only read, and the time starts when you allow it.
4. To stop early, tap **End now**.

**Tip:** Every view by Krama support shows in the **Activity log**.
**Stuck?** Planners see the request but cannot answer it. The page says "Only a host of this wedding can answer."

## How to see who did what
1. Open **Settings**. Tap **Open activity log**. On a computer, pick **Activity log** in the Settings list.
2. Read the list. It shows exports, phone numbers used, role changes, people removed and what Krama support viewed.
3. Tap **Settings** at the top to go back.

**Tip:** The log holds no names or phone numbers. It is kept for a year.
**Stuck?** "Nothing recorded yet." is normal for a new wedding.
