# Personal messages

**What it is:** One ready message for each family, with their own functions, room, pickup and driver in it.
**Why you'd use it:** Before the Sharma-Patel wedding you want to send a save the date to every family. Later you want a thank-you to each one. Each family gets a message with its own details, so nobody has to ask Mausi what time the haldi is.

Krama never sends WhatsApp messages by itself. Each button opens your own WhatsApp or email app with the message ready. You press send. The one exception is **Schedule email**, below.

## How to send a save the date, invitation or thank-you
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Personal messages**.
3. Under **Message**, pick one, such as **Save the date**, **Invitation** or **Thank you**.
4. Under **Send to**, pick **Everyone** or **Not replied yet**.
5. Tap **Preview message** on a family to read it.
6. Tap **Send on WhatsApp**. Your WhatsApp opens. You press send.

Each family has its own row and its own button. Do one family, then the next.

**Tip:** Under **Send by**, pick **Email** and the button becomes **Send by email**. For an invitation, pick a function under **Function**, such as **Haldi**, to talk about just that one.
**Stuck?** If a family shows **No phone or email on file**, tap **Copy link** and paste it into any chat yourself. If a message is in another language and says "needs native review", read it first, or ask someone in the family to check it.

## How to schedule an email
Krama sends these emails itself, at the time you pick. It uses each family's own local time. Only families with an email address get one.

1. Open **Personal messages**, as above.
2. Pick the kind under **Message**.
3. Pick **Everyone** or **Not replied yet** under **Send to**.
4. Under **Schedule email**, pick a **Date**.
5. Set the **Time (their local time)**.
6. Tap **Schedule email**.

You will see how many emails are scheduled. To change your mind, tap **Cancel** with the number waiting, for example **Cancel 12 waiting**.

**Tip:** A family in Dubai gets the email at your chosen time in Dubai, not in India. Pick a time between 08:00 and 20:59.
**Stuck?** Guests cannot reply to these emails. They only open the link inside. Scheduling again replaces the emails still waiting.

## How to turn on the easier RSVP page for some families
The easier page has big text, one question at a time and a Call button. It helps elders who find small screens hard.

1. Open **Personal messages**, as above.
2. Find the box called **Easier-to-read RSVP page**.
3. Tap **Turn on for these families**.

It turns on for the families you see on this page. To turn it off, tap **Turn off**. Move to the next page and do it again for the rest.

**Tip:** To change just one family, tick the box on their row, then tap **Save** on that row.
**Stuck?** Guests can also switch the easier page on for themselves from their own RSVP page.
