# Pass and contacts (guest side)

**What it is:** Two tabs on your RSVP page. **Pass** is your family's check-in code for the door. **Contacts** lists who to call on the day.
**Why you'd use it:** It is the day of the sangeet. Mama ji stands at the gate and the line is long. You open your pass and the family walks in. Later you lose the venue and call the airport pickup number.

## How to show your family pass at the door
1. Open your RSVP link.
2. Tap **Pass**.
3. Show the code at the entrance.
4. To keep a paper copy, tap **Print**.

One pass covers your whole family. Keep it to your family: anyone with the pass can be checked in as you. On the day of a function your link may open on **Pass** by itself.

**Tip:** Open your pass before you reach the gate, and turn up the screen brightness.
**Stuck?** **Pass** shows only after someone in your family says **Going**. If you do not see it, tap **RSVP**, choose **Going** for one function, then tap **Send our reply**. See [[guest-rsvp-page]].

## How to find who to call on the day
1. Open your RSVP link.
2. Tap **Contacts**.
3. Tap a phone number to call.

Each person has a name and a short note, like Airport pickup.

**Tip:** Save the pickup number in your phone before the day.
**Stuck?** **Contacts** shows only when the family has switched a contact on. If you do not see the tab, ask the family.

## For the couple
A contact reaches guests only when you switch it on. It is off by default.
1. Open **Money**, then tap **Vendors**.
2. Open the contact you want to show.
3. Tick **Show to guests on their wedding page**.
4. Type what guests see, in **What guests see (e.g. Airport pickup)**.
5. Tap **Save vendor**.

Guests then see that name and phone on their **Contacts** tab. Pass needs nothing from you. It appears once a guest is coming. See [[headcounts-and-reports]] and [[stays-and-rooms]].

**Tip:** Start with the airport pickup and the hotel desk.
**Stuck?** If guests do not see the contact, check the tick is on, then tap **Save vendor** again.
