# Invitations & reminders

**What it is:** A list of the families who have not replied yet, with a ready message for each one.
**Why you'd use it:** The Sharma-Patel mehndi is in two weeks, and Mausi's family still has not said if they are coming. You remind them in a few taps, from your own phone.

Krama never sends the message by itself. Each button opens your own WhatsApp or email app with the message ready. You press send.

## How to remind families who haven't replied
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Remind the 31 waiting**. The number is how many families are waiting.
3. Under **Send by**, pick **WhatsApp**, **Email** or **Both**.
4. Tap **Remind on WhatsApp** beside a family. For email, tap **Remind by email**.
5. Your own app opens with the message and their RSVP link. You press send.
6. Come back. That family moves out of the list. Do the next one.

The top of the list shows where you are, like "3 of 31". Tap **Skip** to leave a family for later. Tap **Stop for today** when you have done enough.

**Tip:** A family you reminded waits one day before they show here again. Tap **Show them** if you want to see them sooner.
**Stuck?** If a family has no phone or email, you only see **Copy RSVP link**. Tap it, then paste the link into any chat yourself.

## How to set a reply-by date
Only a host or a planner can do this.

1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Set a reply-by date**. If you do not see it, tap **Remind the 31 waiting** instead.
3. Find **Please reply by**.
4. Tap the box and pick the date.
5. Tap **Save**.

The date shows on every family's RSVP page and in the reminder message. It is a gentle nudge. The links keep working after the date.

**Tip:** Pick a date a few weeks before the caterer needs the final count.
**Stuck?** If you cannot see the date box, you are not a host or a planner. Ask one of them to set it.

## How to choose who guests can call
Only a host or a planner can do this. Guests see a **Call** button on the easier-to-read RSVP page.

1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Remind the 31 waiting**.
3. Find **Who guests can call**.
4. Pick a person from the list.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Pick the family member who answers the phone fastest. Their number is then visible to every family with a link.
**Stuck?** The list only shows people who have a phone number in your contacts. Add the number first, then come back. Pick **Nobody (no Call button)** to remove the button.
