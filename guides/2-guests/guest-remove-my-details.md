# Remove my details (guest side)

**What it is:** A button on your RSVP page that deletes you from the guest list. Another page stops the family's emails.
**Why you'd use it:** Your uncle got the link but he is not going to the wedding, and he does not want his number kept. Or you are tired of the reminder emails.

## How to remove your details
1. Open your RSVP link.
2. Scroll down the **RSVP** tab to **Remove my details**.
3. Tap the button with your name. It says Remove, then your name.
4. Tap **Yes, remove**.

Your details and replies are deleted at once, with any room and travel notes. This can't be undone. Tap **Cancel** if you change your mind. A message says "Removed. Thank you."

You need no account. On the other tabs, tap **Remove my details** at the bottom. On the easier-to-read page, tap **Use the full page (also to remove my details)**.

Removing one person leaves the rest of your family on the list. Each person has their own button.

**Tip:** Only want to say you can't come? Do not remove your details. Open your link and tap **Can't** for each function instead.
**Stuck?** If the page asks you to try again, wait a little and tap the button once more. If it still fails, tell the family and they can delete you.

## How to stop emails
1. Open an email from the family.
2. Scroll to the end and tap the link after "To stop these emails, open:".
3. Tap **Stop emails**.

The page says **Done**. You will get no more emails about this wedding. Your RSVP link still works.

Krama never sends WhatsApp messages by itself. A WhatsApp message comes from the family's own phone, so ask them to stop it.

**Tip:** Stopping emails does not remove you. Your RSVP link still works.
**Stuck?** If you cannot find the link, look at the very end of the email. A WhatsApp message has no link, so ask the family to stop it.

## For the couple
A host can also delete a guest from the guest list. See [[guest-list]]. When a guest removes themselves, your activity log shows it, with nothing that says who.
