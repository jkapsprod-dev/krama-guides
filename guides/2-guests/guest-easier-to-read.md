# Easier-to-read RSVP (guest side)

**What it is:** A bigger, simpler RSVP page. It asks one question at a time, with big buttons.
**Why you'd use it:** Nani gets the link on her old phone. The small words and tabs confuse her. With this page she answers one question at a time.

## How to turn on easier reading
1. Open your RSVP link.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the **RSVP** tab.
3. Tap **Easier to read**.

The page now shows big words. This phone remembers your choice. To go back, tap **Turn off easier reading** at the bottom. The family may also have switched it on for you.

**Tip:** To see the normal page once, tap **Use the full page (also to remove my details)**.
**Stuck?** If you do not see **Easier to read**, scroll right to the bottom of the **RSVP** tab.

## How to answer one question at a time
1. Read the question, then tap **Yes, we will come** or **No, we cannot come**.
2. If you said yes, set how many will come with the plus and minus buttons. Then tap **Next**.
3. Pick the food your family eats, or tap **Skip**.
4. On **Check your answers**, tap **Change** next to anything that is wrong.
5. Tap **Send my answer**.

A thank-you screen shows. You can open the page again to change your answer. If the family added a phone number, a big **Call** button shows with their name.

**Tip:** Tap **Back** if you pressed the wrong answer.
**Stuck?** **Send my answer** stays grey until every function has an answer. Tap **Change** next to the one you missed.

## How to hear it read aloud
1. Open the easier-to-read page.
2. Tap **Read aloud** under the question.
3. To stop, tap **Stop reading**.

It uses your phone's own voice. Nothing is sent anywhere. It speaks English only for now.

**Tip:** To see the dates, venues and travel, tap **Schedule, stay and travel**.
**Stuck?** If you see no **Read aloud** button, your phone or browser has no voice. Open the link in your phone's main browser.

## For the couple
You can switch this page on for a family. Open [[personal-messages]] and tap **Turn on for these families**. To add the **Call** button, pick a contact under **Who guests can call** in [[invitations-and-reminders]]. That person's number is then visible to any family with a link.
