# RSVP page (guest side)

**What it is:** The page behind the link the family sent you. You say who is coming, see the schedule and fix your details. You need no app and no account.
**Why you'd use it:** Mausi gets the link on WhatsApp. She wants to tell the family she is coming to the haldi and the sangeet, but not the reception.

## How to reply for your family
1. Open the link the family sent you.
2. Under each function, tap **Going** or **Can't**.
3. If you tapped **Going**, use the plus and minus buttons beside **Guests coming**.
4. Pick a **Meal preference**.
5. Tap **Send our reply**.

A card then says you are on the list. It has **Add to calendar** and **Your pass** buttons. You can change your reply any time with the same link.

**Tip:** Open **Travel details (optional)** on the **RSVP** tab and add your train or flight. It helps the family plan your pickup.
**Stuck?** If the page asks you to choose, you missed a function: tap **Going** or **Can't** on each one.

## How to see the schedule and add it to your calendar
1. Open your link.
2. Tap **Schedule**.
3. Tap **Add all my functions**.
4. To add one function, tap **Add to calendar** under it.
5. To see the venue, tap **Open in Maps**.

**Add all my functions** skips any function you said **Can't** to. Each function also has a **Google Calendar** button.

**Tip:** On the day, tap **Open in Maps** to find the venue.
**Stuck?** If fewer functions are added, you said **Can't** to the rest.

## How to fix your details
1. Open your link.
2. Tap **My details**.
3. Fix your **Name** or your **Meal preference**.
4. Tap **Save my details**.

Only what you change is saved. If you see **Phone**, **WhatsApp number** and **Email** boxes, you can fix those too.

**Tip:** Fix your name now, so your card and pass are right.
**Stuck?** If the page says the wedding is no longer taking replies, or the link isn't working, ask the family for a new link.

Only your own family's page opens from your link. The family uses your name, phone and replies only to plan the wedding, and everything is deleted after it. Tap **Read the full privacy notice** to read more.

## How to read the page in your language
1. Open the link the family sent you.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Tap **Language**.
4. Pick your language. The page changes at once.

**Tip:** Your choice stays on this phone, so the page opens in your language next time too.
**Stuck?** Some words still show in English? Languages other than English have a **BETA** mark. People who speak them are still checking the words.
