# Send RSVP links

**What it is:** Each family gets one private link that covers every function they are invited to. You send it from your own WhatsApp or email app, and you press send. Krama sends nothing by itself.
**Why you'd use it:** Meena Bua's family lives in Pune. You send her one link on WhatsApp, and she tells you who is coming to the haldi, the sangeet and the wedding.

## How to invite a guest to functions
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the guest's name.
3. Under **Functions**, find the function, like Haldi.
4. Tap **Invite**.

**Tip:** To invite many guests at once, see [[guest-list]]. To take a guest off a function, tap **Remove** on the same row.
**Stuck?** **Invite** only adds the guest to the function. It does not send anything. Send the link as shown below.

## How to send a family their link
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the guest's name.
3. Tap **Message on WhatsApp**.
4. WhatsApp opens with a ready message and the family's own link.
5. You press send in WhatsApp.

Prefer email? Tap **Send by email**. Your email app opens, and you press send. Want to paste it somewhere else? Tap **Copy RSVP link**.

**Tip:** Send the link once for each family. It covers everyone in the family. Guests need no app and no account. They open it on any phone.
**Stuck?** You see no send button? The guest has no phone, WhatsApp number or email. Tap **Edit** and add one.

## How to stop a link shared by mistake
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the guest's name.
3. Tap **Reset link**.
4. The old link stops working at once.
5. Send the new link, as shown above.

**Tip:** Reset a link only when it went to the wrong person. Anyone who has the old link must get the new one.
**Stuck?** **Reset link** shows only for a guest who is in a family. Put the guest in a family first. See [[add-a-guest]].

## How to record a reply for a guest
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the guest's name.
3. Under **Functions**, find the function. Tap the reply box. It first says Invited.
4. Pick **Attending**, **Declined** or **Maybe**.
5. If they are coming, type how many people in the number box beside it.

**Tip:** Use this when someone replies by phone call or at a family dinner. The family can still change their own reply with their link.
**Stuck?** Can't change the reply? Viewers can only look. A helper can change replies only for the guests they are given.
