# Headcounts and reports

**What it is:** The number of people coming to each function and what they eat, plus ready sheets you can hand to the caterer, the hotel or your drivers.
**Why you'd use it:** The caterer for the Sharma-Patel sangeet calls and asks, "How many plates, and how many Jain?" You open one page and read out the numbers. Then you send him the sheet.

Reports are for hosts, planners and family. Nothing about money is in any sheet.

## How to see who's coming to each function
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Headcounts**.
3. Find the function card, such as the sangeet.
4. Read the big **Headcount** number.
5. Under it, read **Guests attending**, **Declined** and **Awaiting**.
6. Read **Meals** to see how many eat each kind of food.

Each family is counted once. The numbers move as families reply.

**Tip:** Tap **Remind the 33 waiting** on the Guests page to bring the **Awaiting** number down before you call the caterer.
**Stuck?** If it says **No invitations yet.**, you have not invited anyone to a function. Invite guests first. See [[send-rsvp-links]].

## How to send the caterer a sheet
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Reports**.
3. Find **Caterer sheet**.
4. Tap **Excel**. You can also tap **CSV** or **PDF**.
5. Open the file from your phone's downloads.
6. Send it to the caterer yourself, on WhatsApp or email.

The sheet has the headcount and the meal split for every function.

**Tip:** On the **Headcounts** page, tap **Download CSV** for the same numbers in one tap.
**Stuck?** PDFs use a basic font, so names in other scripts can show as ?. Use **Excel** or **CSV** for those.

## How to download the transport manifest or RSVP by side
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Reports**.
3. Find **Transport manifest** or **RSVP by side**.
4. Tap **Excel**. You can also tap **CSV** or **PDF**.
5. Open the file and share it yourself.

**Transport manifest** lists arrivals and departures in time order, with who is picking up. **RSVP by side** shows coming, can't come and waiting for each function, split by bride's side and groom's side.

**Tip:** Send the transport manifest to your drivers a day before. The **Rooming list** here is for the hotel. See [[stays-and-rooms]].
**Stuck?** If you do not see **Transport manifest**, **Stays & Travel** is not switched on for your wedding. A host can turn it on.
