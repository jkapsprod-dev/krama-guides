# Guest list

**What it is:** All your guests in one list. You can search it, filter it, and change many guests at once.
**Why you'd use it:** Mausi calls to ask if her family is on the list. You search her name and see her family, her functions and her reply in seconds.

## How to find a guest
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the search box. It says **Search by name, relation, family or phone**.
3. Type part of a name, a relation or a family name.
4. Tap **Search**.
5. Tap the guest's name to see their details.

**Tip:** To search by phone, type the whole number. Part of a number finds nothing.
**Stuck?** **No guests match** means nothing fits. Try a shorter name. Or open **More filters** and tap **Clear filters**.

## How to filter by side or reply
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Bride's side** or **Groom's side**.
3. Tap **Awaiting** to see who has not replied. Or tap **Attending** to see who is coming. Or tap **Declined** to see who can't come.
4. To narrow it more, tap **More filters**.
5. Pick a function, a meal, a tag or a city. Then tap **Apply**.
6. Tap **Clear filters** to start again.

**Tip:** The three numbers at the top are filters too. They are **Coming**, **Can't come** and **Waiting**. Tap one, and tap it again to undo.
**Stuck?** The list looks too short? A filter is still on. Open **More filters** and tap **Clear filters**.

## How to invite many guests at once
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tick the box beside each guest you want.
3. In the bar that opens, tap **Choose a function**.
4. Pick the function, like Haldi.
5. Tap **Invite**.

**Tip:** Tick a family's name to pick the whole family. On a phone, tick **Select all on this page** to pick every guest on the page. This only adds them to the function. It sends no message, so see [[send-rsvp-links]] next.
**Stuck?** You see no boxes? Only hosts, planners and family can change the list. Helpers see only the guests they are given. Viewers can only look, and phone numbers show with some digits hidden.

## How to tag many guests
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tick the box beside each guest you want.
3. In the bar, type a word in the box **Tag, e.g. pune**.
4. Tap **Add tag**.

**Tip:** Use tags for groups you will need again, like pune, college or train. Later, open **More filters** and pick a tag in **Any tag**.
**Stuck?** **Add tag** stays grey until you type a tag.

## How to delete many guests
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tick the box beside each guest you want to remove.
3. Tap **Delete**.
4. Read the box. It says how many guests will go.
5. Tap **Yes, delete**.

**Tip:** To delete just one guest, open them and tap **Delete**.
**Stuck?** Tapped **Delete** by mistake? Tap **Cancel**. After **Yes, delete**, the guests and their replies are gone for good.
