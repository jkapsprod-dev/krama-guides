# Arrivals & departures

**What it is:** A day-by-day list of who is coming in and going out, by train, flight, car or bus, and who is picking them up.
**Why you'd use it:** Chacha's train reaches Ahmedabad at 5 am the day before the haldi. You note the train, give the pickup to your cousin, and she ticks him off when he arrives.

## How to add a train or a flight
1. Open **Stays & Travel**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Tap the **Arrivals & departures** tab.
3. Tap **Add travel**.
4. Choose the **Guest**.
5. Fill in **Arriving or leaving**, **By**, **Flight / train number**, **Date** and **Time**.
6. Tap **Save**.

Families can also add their own arrival on their RSVP page. Those lines say **From RSVP**.

**Tip:** Leave **Time** empty if you don't know it yet. You can add it later.
**Stuck?** If the guest isn't in the **Guest** list, add them in **Guests** first.

## How to give someone a pickup
1. Open the **Arrivals & departures** tab.
2. Find the guest's line and tap **Edit**.
3. Switch on **Needs a pickup**.
4. Choose a name in **Picked up by**.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** For a hired driver, use the **Driver** box on the same form, or plan runs in [[pickups]].
**Stuck?** A line that says "Pickup not assigned" still needs a name in **Picked up by**.

## How to mark a guest arrived
1. Open the **Arrivals & departures** tab.
2. Find the guest's line under the right day.
3. Tap the **Expected** button next to their name.
4. It changes to **Arrived**.

**Tip:** Tapped too soon? Tap **Arrived** again to put it back to **Expected**.
**Stuck?** Only arrivals have this button. A helper sees it only on pickups given to them.

## How to see only your pickups
1. Open the **Arrivals & departures** tab.
2. Find **Show**.
3. Tap **My pickups**.
4. Tap **Everyone** to see all lines again.

**Tip:** Helpers start on **My pickups**, so they see only their own jobs. To call a guest, tap Call under their name.
**Stuck?** If you see "No pickups assigned to you.", nobody has put your name in **Picked up by** yet. Ask a host.
