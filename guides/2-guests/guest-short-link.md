# Short link (guest side)

**What it is:** A short web address for the wedding, like a name tag. It shows only a few things the family chose to share.
**Why you'd use it:** A cousin in Dubai hears about the wedding at a party. The family says "just type our short link", and she sees the names and the month at once.

## How to see the wedding on the short link
1. Open the short link the family gave you.
2. Read the names, the month and the city at the top.
3. Look under **Functions** to see each function and its day.
4. To reply, open the personal link the family sent you.

The short link never shows guest names, phone numbers, addresses, venues or times. Your own link has the full details and your RSVP.

**Tip:** Save your personal link too. The short link is only a first look.
**Stuck?** If the short link shows a "page not found" screen, the family has not switched it on yet, or the address has a typo. Ask them to check it.

## For the couple
The short link is off until a host switches it on. Only a host can do this.
1. Open **More**, then tap **Codes & planner**.
2. Under **Wedding code**, set your code if you have none.
3. Under **What the short link shows**, type the **Names to show**.
4. Under **Show on the short link**, tick what to show: **The names above**, **Month and city**, **Function names and dates**.
5. Tap **Save**.
6. Tap **Copy short link** and send it to your guests.

Whatever you leave unticked stays hidden. If nothing is ticked, the short link shows nothing. The video for this part is in [[wedding-id-and-code]].

**Tip:** Start with only **Month and city** ticked. Add more when you are sure.
**Stuck?** If a guest sees "page not found", check that you set a wedding code and tapped **Save**.
