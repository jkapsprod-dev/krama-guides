# Add a guest

**What it is:** Add one guest, or one whole family, to your list. Each family gets one RSVP link.
**Why you'd use it:** Your cousin phones to say her husband's parents are coming too. You add them in a minute and send their link.

## How to add one guest
1. Tap the (+) button at the bottom.
2. Tap **Add a guest**.
3. Type the **Full name**.
4. Type the **Phone**. Indian numbers need no +91.
5. Under **Invite to**, untick any function they are not invited to.
6. Tap **Save guest**.

On a computer, open **Guests** and tap **Add guest** instead of steps 1 and 2.

**Tip:** After you save, a card shows **Message on WhatsApp** and **Copy RSVP link**. If you typed an email, it also shows **Send by email**. You press send in your own app. To add the next guest, tap **Add another**.
**Stuck?** Red words under a box tell you what to fix. A name is needed. A phone needs 10 digits, or start with + for another country. If it says the plan is frozen, a host or planner can lift the freeze in **Plan**, under **Freezes**.

## How to put guests in one family
1. Tap the (+) button, then tap **Add a guest**.
2. Type the **Full name**.
3. Tap **Family (for RSVPs)**.
4. Pick the family, like Sharma family, Pune. Pick **A new family** if there is none yet.
5. Tap **Save guest**.

**Tip:** One RSVP link covers the whole family. So put parents, children and grandparents who travel together in one family.
**Stuck?** Picked **A new family**? Then type a **Family name** so you can find it later.

## How to add children's counts
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap a guest in the family.
3. Find **Children**. Tap **Edit**.
4. Type the numbers for **Children (3 to 12)** and **Infants (under 3)**.
5. Tap **Save**.

Adding a new family? The same two boxes are on the **Add a guest** form, below **Family name**.

**Tip:** Children are counted, not named. Krama does not keep personal details of children, and the caterer still gets the right count.
**Stuck?** Use numbers from 0 to 20. Do you see old children listed by name? A host or planner can tap **Convert to counts** on **Guests**.

## How to handle a possible duplicate
1. Fill in the form and tap **Save guest**.
2. Read the box **This might be a duplicate**. It says who matches and why.
3. Same person? Tap **View {name}** to open them, and stop there.
4. Someone new? Tap **Add anyway**.

**Tip:** Krama checks for the same phone, or the same name in the same city.
**Stuck?** A husband and wife often share one phone. They are two people, so tap **Add anyway**.

## How to change a guest's details
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap the guest's name.
3. Tap **Edit**.
4. Change what you need.
5. Tap **Save guest**.

**Tip:** Tick or untick a function under **Invite to** to change who is invited.
**Stuck?** No **Edit** button? Only hosts, planners and family can edit guests.
