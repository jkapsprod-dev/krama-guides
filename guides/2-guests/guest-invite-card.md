# Invite card (guest side)

**What it is:** Your own invitation card. It has your name, the wedding and the functions you are invited to.
**Why you'd use it:** Bua wants the card in her gallery so she can show it to the whole family at home, and print one for the fridge.

## How to save your invite card
1. Open the card link the family sent you.
2. Look at your name and the functions on the card.
3. Tap **Save as image** to keep a picture on your phone.

Want a PDF to print or share? Tap **Save as PDF** instead. Then choose Save as PDF in the print box.

**Tip:** After you save the image, share it from your phone's gallery. It shows only the functions you are invited to.
**Stuck?** If you see "Could not make the image", tap **Save as PDF** instead.

## How to reply from the card
1. Open your card.
2. Tap **Reply to this invitation**.
3. Tap **Going** or **Can't** for each function.
4. Tap **Send our reply**.

**Tip:** You can change your reply later, from the same link.
**Stuck?** If the card says the link isn't working, ask the family for a new link.
