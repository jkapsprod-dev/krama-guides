# Invite QR

**What it is:** A QR code you print on your patrika. Relatives scan it and send their own name and phone. You check each one before they join your guest list.
**Why you'd use it:** The Sharma-Patel families have many far cousins, and nobody has all their numbers. You print one QR on the card. Mausi scans it and sends her details. You do not type anything.

Only a host, a planner or family can use the couple side. Helpers and viewers do not see it.

## How to make an invite QR
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Invite QR**.
3. Tap **Create the QR**.
4. Tap **Print** to print it for your patrika.
5. Or tap **Copy link** and share the link yourself.

You press send on any message. Krama sends nothing.

**Tip:** Tap **Switch off** when you have all the names you need. Nobody can send details until you tap **Switch on** again.
**Stuck?** If the QR was shared by mistake, tap **New QR**, then confirm. The old QR and link stop working at once. Anything already printed needs to be replaced.

## How to add the people who scanned it
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Invite QR**. The number shows how many are waiting.
3. Read the name, phone, side and city on a request.
4. Tap **Add to guest list**.
5. If you do not know them, tap **Reject**. Then tap **Reject** again to confirm.

If the person is already on your list, you see a note. Then you can tap **Add this phone to the existing guest**, or **Add as a new guest**. If the phone number is already on your list, you can only reject.

A new guest gets their own family. They are not invited to any function yet. Invite them from the guest list.

**Tip:** Check the note under the name. It tells you if Krama found someone like them already.
**Stuck?** Reject deletes their details for good. Only tap it when you are sure.

## How to send your details
This part is for you, the guest. You scanned a QR on a wedding card. The family will check your details and add you to the guest list.

1. Scan the QR with your phone camera. Or open the link the family sent you.
2. Type **Your name**.
3. Type your **Phone number**. Indian numbers need no +91.
4. Pick **Which side are you from?** if you know. Add your **City** if you like.
5. Tap **Send my details**.
6. You see **Thank you**. You are done.

**Tip:** You can write a short message under **Anything to tell the family (optional)**.
**Stuck?** If it says the link is not working, the family may have switched it off or replaced it. Ask them for the new one.
