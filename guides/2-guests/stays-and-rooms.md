# Stays & rooms

**What it is:** A place to list the hotels and homes where guests will sleep, add their rooms, and put each family in a room.
**Why you'd use it:** The Mehta family is coming from Pune for the Sharma-Patel wedding. Mausi wants to know her room number before she packs. You can tell her in one tap.

## How to add a hotel or a home
1. Open **Stays & Travel**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Tap **Add a stay**.
3. Type the **Name**, like Hotel Riverfront Residency.
4. Fill in the **Address**, **Check-in** and **Check-out**.
5. Tap **Save stay**.

**Tip:** A farmhouse or a family home counts too. Add it the same way, then give it rooms.
**Stuck?** If you can't see **Add a stay**, your role may only let you read. Hosts, planners and family can add stays.

## How to add rooms
1. Open **Stays & Travel**.
2. Find **Add rooms** under the **Room map**. With no rooms yet, the form is already open. If it is closed, tap **Add rooms** to open it.
3. Type the first and last room numbers in **From** and **To**, like 101 and 110.
4. Fill in **Floor**, **Type** and **Sleeps**. Sleeps is how many people fit in one room.
5. Tap **Add rooms** again to save them.

You can add up to 200 rooms at once.

**Tip:** Add each floor on its own, so the **Room map** groups rooms by floor.
**Stuck?** If the rooms went to the wrong hotel, pick the right one in the **Stay** box, tap **Apply**, and add them again.

## How to mark a family that needs a room
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap a guest from that family.
3. Find **Stay & travel** on the guest's page.
4. Switch on **Needs a room**.
5. Open **Stays & Travel**. The family now shows under **Waiting for a room**.

**Tip:** The switch covers the whole family, so you do it once.
**Stuck?** If you tapped it by mistake, tap **Undo** in the message that appears. You have 10 seconds.

## How to put a family in a room
1. Open **Stays & Travel**.
2. Under **Waiting for a room**, tap **Assign** next to the family.
3. Tap a free room on the **Room map**.
4. Check **Who's staying**, **Check-in** and **Check-out**.
5. Tap **Assign**.
6. If Krama shows a warning and you are happy, tap **Assign anyway**.

Krama warns you when a room is too small. It also warns you when a guest already has a room on those nights.

**Tip:** Rooms are coloured by side: **Bride's side**, **Groom's side**, **Both sides** or **Available**. You can see who is next to whom at a glance.
**Stuck?** To move a family, tap their room, tap **Take out of room**, then assign them again.

## How to print the rooming list for the hotel
1. Open **Stays & Travel**.
2. Tap **Rooming list**.
3. Tap **Print** for paper or a PDF.
4. Or tap **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet.
5. Send it to the hotel yourself. Krama does not send it.

**Tip:** Send the list to the hotel desk instead of giving them an account.
**Stuck?** The list is for the hotel you are looking at. To print another hotel, pick it in the **Stay** box, tap **Apply**, then tap **Rooming list**.

## How to mark a destination wedding
Hosts and planners only.

1. Open **Stays & Travel**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. At the top of **Stays & rooms**, find **Destination wedding**.
3. Turn the switch on.
4. Open **Tasks** to see the new destination checklist, like the room block, pickups and welcome kits.

**Tip:** The first time you switch it on, your budget also gets a "Travel and stay" line. Set its amount in **Money**.
**Stuck?** No switch? Only hosts and planners see it. Turning it off later keeps the tasks and the budget line, so delete them yourself if you don't need them.
