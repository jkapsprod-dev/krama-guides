# Upload QR (guests add photos)

**What it is:** A QR code that lets any guest add photos to your wedding gallery, with no sign-in.
**Why you'd use it:** Your college friends took hundreds of photos at the sangeet. Put the QR on the tables, and the photos land in your gallery without anyone chasing them.

**Who can see this:** Only a host or planner can make or change the QR. A guest who scans it sees only the photos they added. Everyone in your wedding sees all the photos in **Photos**, and hosts can delete any photo. Photos show no money.

## How to make a photo QR for guests
1. Open **Photos**.
2. Tap **Upload QR** at the top.
3. Tap **Create the upload QR**.
4. Tap **Print** to print it for the venue or the invitation. Or tap **Copy link** and send the link yourself.

**Tip:** The printed page shows your wedding name and "Scan to add your photos". Put it on tables and at the photo booth.
**Stuck?** No **Upload QR** button? Only a host or planner sees it, and the photo gallery must be on. Ask a host. Photos added with the QR use up your gallery storage.

## How to switch it off or replace it
1. Open **Photos** and tap **Upload QR**.
2. To pause it, tap **Switch off**. Nobody can add photos until you tap **Switch on**.
3. To start fresh, tap **New QR**.
4. Read the question and tap **New QR** again to confirm.
5. Print or copy the new QR.

**Tip:** Switch it off after the wedding week so no new photos arrive.
**Stuck?** Old printed QR codes stop working at once after **New QR**. Print the new one and replace them.

## How a guest adds photos with the QR
1. Scan the QR with your phone camera, or open the link you were sent.
2. Type **Your name (optional)** if you like.
3. Pick the function under **Album**.
4. Tap **Add photos** and choose your photos.
5. Wait for "photos added". You will see them under **Your photos**.
6. To remove one, tap **Delete photo** on it, then tap **Delete photo** again to confirm.

**Tip:** Use the same phone and browser later. That is how you see your own photos again.
**Stuck?** You see "Link not found"? Ask the family for a new QR. You see "This wedding isn't taking photos right now"? The family has paused it.
