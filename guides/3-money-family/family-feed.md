# Family feed

**What it is:** A private place where your wedding team shares updates and photos, and reacts to each other's posts.
**Why you'd use it:** Mausi keeps asking the haldi time, and the cousins want to see the mehndi photos. One post reaches everyone, with no long WhatsApp scroll.

**Who can see this:** Everyone in your wedding can read the feed. Guests never see it. Host, planner, family and helpers can post. Viewers can read, react and report, but they can't post. The feed shows no money.

## How to post an update or photos
1. Open **Family feed**. On a phone, it sits under **More**.
2. Tap the box that says "Share an update with the family…" and type your update.
3. To add photos, tap **Add photos** and choose up to 4.
4. Check the small pictures. Tap the X on a picture to remove it.
5. Tap **Post**.

**Tip:** Photos are made smaller before they go up, and the location is removed. Use the **Photos** section when you want full quality.
**Stuck?** No box to write in? Viewers can't post, and a read-only wedding can't take posts. Ask a host. If a photo won't add, use JPEG, PNG or WebP.

## How to react to a post
1. Open **Family feed**.
2. Find the post.
3. Tap one of the five buttons under it: **Heart**, **Clap**, **Folded hands**, **Laugh** or **Celebrate**.
4. To take your reaction back, tap the same button again.

**Tip:** You get one reaction per post. Tap a different button to change it.
**Stuck?** The buttons look grey? The wedding may be read-only, so nothing can change right now.

## How to report or remove a post
1. Open **Family feed** and find the post.
2. If someone else wrote it, tap **Report**. The button then says **Reported**, and the hosts see a report count on the post.
3. If you wrote it, tap **Delete post**. If you are a host and it is someone else's post, tap **Remove post**.
4. Read the question and tap the same button again to confirm.

**Tip:** Reporting twice does nothing extra. The host decides what to do, and the author is not told who removed the post.
**Stuck?** No **Remove post** button? Only a host can remove other people's posts. Everyone else can delete only their own.
