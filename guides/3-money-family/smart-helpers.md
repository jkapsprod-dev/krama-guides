# Smart helpers

**What it is:** Four small helpers that use public information, with one switch each: weather alerts, festival clashes, the gold price and Snap a bill.
**Why you'd use it:** Your uncle does not want any app sending a photo anywhere. You switch Snap a bill off, and keep the weather alerts on.

**Who can change this:** Hosts and planners. Settings is theirs, so family, helpers and viewers do not get it in their menu. None of the helpers sends a guest name, phone or email anywhere.

**What is on to begin with:**
- **Weather alerts for open-air functions:** on. It sends only a city to a public forecast service.
- **Festival and long-weekend clashes:** on. It uses a holiday list inside Krama. Nothing is sent.
- **Gold price on the jewellery budget line:** on. It fetches the current price. Nothing about your wedding is sent.
- **Snap a bill:** off. You switch it on yourself.

## How to switch a smart helper on or off
1. Open **More**, then **Settings**. On a computer, click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Tap **Smart helpers**. On a computer, it is in the same list on the left of **Settings**.
3. Read the line under each helper. It says what the helper does and what it sends.
4. Tick a box to switch a helper on. Untick it to switch it off.
5. Tap **Save**.
6. Wait for **Saved.** to show.

**Tip:** Snap a bill is the only helper that starts off. Switch it on here before you look for it on the expense page.
**Stuck?** A box is greyed out and says **Switched off by Krama for now.** Krama has paused that helper for everyone, and you cannot switch it on. If the whole list is greyed out and there is no **Save** button, you are not a host or a planner. Ask one of them.
