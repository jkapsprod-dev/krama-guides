# Shagun

**What it is:** A register of the envelopes and gifts your family gives and receives, by function and by family.
**Why you'd use it:** After the wedding, someone asks who gave the silver tray and who gave the ₹11,000 envelope. You also need to know whom to thank, and what to give back at their own weddings.

**Who can see this:** Shagun amounts are visible to the host, the planner and family only. Helpers and viewers never see Shagun, and guests never do. Krama only records the envelope or gift. It never holds or moves money.

## How to note an envelope or a gift
1. Open **Shagun**. On a phone, it sits under **More**.
2. Tap **Add an entry**.
3. Under **What happened**, choose **We received** or **We gave**. Then under **Type**, choose **Cash or envelope** or **Gift**.
4. Pick the family under **Family on the guest list**. If they are not on your list, choose **Someone not on the list** and type a **Name**.
5. For an envelope, type the **Amount (₹)**, for example 5,100. For a gift, say **What is it**. The **Value (₹), optional** is up to you.
6. Pick the **Function** if you like. Then tap **Save entry**.

**Tip:** You can also tap the **(+)** button and choose **Add a shagun entry**. To fix a mistake, tap the row, change it and save again.
**Stuck?** You can't see **Add an entry**? Your wedding may be read-only, or your role may not allow edits. Ask a host.

## How to see shagun by function or family
1. Open **Shagun**. The three boxes at the top show **Received**, **Given** and **Thank-yous to send**.
2. Under **Show**, tap **By function** to group entries by function.
3. Tap **By family** to group them by family. Each group shows its own total.
4. Tap **All** to see one plain list.
5. Under **Direction**, choose **Received** or **Given** to see only one side.

**Tip:** Shagun opens on **By family**. Once your functions have started and thank-yous are waiting, it opens on **Thank-yous**.
**Stuck?** An entry shows under **Not linked to a function**? Tap the row and pick a **Function**.

## How to thank someone
1. Open **Shagun** and tap **Thank-yous** under **Show**.
2. Find the family and tap **Draft a message**.
3. Read the message. Make it yours before you send it.
4. Tap **Copy message**.
5. Open your own WhatsApp or SMS app, paste the message and press send yourself.
6. Back in Krama, tap **Mark thanked**.

**Tip:** Add the family's own words, like a line about the blessings they gave at the haldi. The draft is only a start.
**Stuck?** Tapped **Mark thanked** by mistake? Tap **Undo** next to it. Krama never sends the message for you. You press send in your own app.

## How to export your shagun list
1. Open **Shagun**.
2. Tap **Export CSV**.
3. Open the file in your spreadsheet app.

**Tip:** Amounts come as plain numbers, so your spreadsheet can add them up.
**Stuck?** No file arrived? Check your phone's downloads folder. Only the host, planner and family can export.
