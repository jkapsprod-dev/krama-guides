# Money (Due)

**What it is:** The **Due** tab in **Money** lists every payment you still have to make, so nothing is missed.
**Why you'd use it:** The caterer wants the second instalment before the sangeet, and Mausi's family asks if it is paid. You open **Due**, see it, and tap **Mark paid**.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family see all money and every amount. Helpers and viewers never see it. There is no **Money** item in their menu. Krama only records what your family has already paid. It does not hold, move or pay money.

## How to see what is due
1. Open **Money**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Stay on the **Due** tab. It opens first.
3. Read **Upcoming payments**. The payments sit in three groups: **Overdue**, **Next 14 days** and **Later**.
4. Look at the row at the top. It shows **Planned**, **Booked**, **Paid** and **Still to pay**.
5. Scroll to **Who’s contributing**. It shows how much each family or person has paid.

**Tip:** Tap a payment to open the full expense, with its vendor and all its payments.
**Stuck?** If the list says nothing is due, no payment has a due date yet. Open an expense and use **Schedule an instalment**, or add a due date when you add the expense.

## How to mark a payment paid
1. Open **Money** and stay on the **Due** tab.
2. Find the payment under **Upcoming payments** and tap **Mark paid**.
3. Check **Amount paid** and **Paid on**. Both are filled in for you. Change them if needed.
4. Pick **How** you paid. Then pick **Who paid**. If you choose **Someone else**, type **Their name**.
5. Add a **Reference** or a **Receipt (optional)** if you have one.
6. Tap **Save payment**. You see "Noted", and the payment leaves the list.

**Tip:** The sheet remembers how and who paid last time, so most payments are two taps.
**Stuck?** If you paid less than was due, type the smaller amount. The rest stays due. If you see no **Mark paid** button, the wedding may be read-only. Ask a host.

## How to filter by function or side
1. Open **Money**.
2. Under the top row, find **Function**. Tap one function, like Haldi. Tap **Whole wedding** to see all.
3. Find **Side**. Tap **Bride’s side**, **Groom’s side** or **Shared**. Tap **Both families** to see all.
4. Read the list and the top row again. They now show only that function or side.

**Tip:** Use both rows together, like Haldi plus **Bride’s side**, to see what one family still owes for one function.
**Stuck?** The **Function** row only shows functions that have an expense. Link an expense to a function and it appears. The filters are hidden on the **Sides** tab.

## How to export
1. On a computer, open **Money**.
2. Tap **Export**, at the top right.
3. A file called budget.csv downloads.
4. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Each expense has its vendor, category, function, side, total agreed, paid and still to pay.

**Tip:** Amounts are plain numbers, so a spreadsheet can add them up. Use this file to share with your CA.
**Stuck?** **Export** is not on a phone screen, and it shows only after you add an expense. On a phone, use **CSV** on the **Sides** tab, or open Krama on a computer.
