# Outfits

**What it is:** A list of who is wearing what to each function, with the colour, the tailor and the fitting dates.
**Why you'd use it:** Three bridesmaids all turn up in maroon for the sangeet, and the tailor still has the lehengas half done.

**Who can see this:** Only hosts, planners and family. Helpers and viewers do not see **Outfits** in the menu, and the page will not open for them. There is no money on this page.

## How to add an outfit
1. Open **Outfits**. On a phone it is under **More**, in **Keepsakes**.
2. Tap **Add an outfit**.
3. Under **Who is it for?**, pick someone by name, a member of your wedding, or a guest on the list. Then fill in the name.
4. Pick the **Function**, type the **Outfit** (for example Silk lehenga) and pick the **Main colour**.
5. Tap **Save outfit**.
6. On the next page, tap **Add a photo** to add up to 3 pictures of the outfit.

**Tip:** Tick **In the bridal party** for the bride's group. Krama only checks colours for them.
**Stuck?** "This person already has an outfit for that function" means there is one already. Open it from the list. You cannot change the person later. To do that, delete the outfit and add a new one.

## How to track fittings and pick-ups
1. Open **Outfits** and tap the outfit, or tap **Add an outfit**.
2. Fill in **Fitting date** and **Alteration or pick-up date**.
3. Keep **Remind us with tasks** ticked. Each date becomes a task in **Tasks** and on **Home**.
4. Under **Who looks after the tasks**, pick a person.
5. Tap **Save outfit**.
6. Back on **Outfits**, tap **Fittings and alterations** to see every date from today, soonest first.

**Tip:** Add the **Stylist or tailor** too, so everyone knows who to call. The tasks are due at 9 am on each date.
**Stuck?** If no task appears, check that **Remind us with tasks** is ticked and that you saved. "No fittings or alterations coming up" means every date is in the past.

## How to spot two people in the same colour
1. Open **Outfits** and tap **Add an outfit**, or open an outfit you already have.
2. Tick **In the bridal party**.
3. Pick the **Main colour** from the list.
4. Tap **Save outfit**.
5. Back on the **By function** view, look for a note at the top that starts with **Same colour**. It names the people and the function.
6. Tap one of the marked rows and change the **Main colour**.

**Tip:** Each row in a clash also has a **Same colour** tag, so you can find it fast.
**Stuck?** The check only covers bridal party people in the same function. It skips **Other**, because two typed colours may still look alike.
