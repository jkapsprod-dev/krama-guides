# Photos

**What it is:** A photo gallery with one album for every function, plus an album called **Other**. Photos are kept at full quality.
**Why you'd use it:** Your cousin took the best sangeet photos on her phone, and the photographer's link expires. Put everything in one place, then download the lot after the wedding.

**Who can see this:** Everyone in your wedding can open **Photos** and add photos. Host, planner and family can move or delete any photo. Helpers can move or delete only the photos they added. Guests don't see this screen. Photos show no money.

## How to add photos to a function's album
1. Open **Photos**. On a phone, it sits under **More**.
2. Under **Album**, pick the function. It starts on the function nearest to today.
3. Tap **Add photos** and choose your photos. On a phone you can also use the camera.
4. Wait while it says "Uploading 1 of 5". Keep the page open.
5. When you see "5 photos added", tap the album to see them.

**Tip:** Photos go up at full quality, with the location removed. You can choose up to 100 at a time. JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC work, up to 50 MB each.
**Stuck?** You see "The photo gallery isn't on"? Ask a host to add the photo gallery. You see "The gallery is full"? Delete a few photos, or ask a host for more storage.

## How to move or delete a photo
1. Open **Photos** and tap the album.
2. Tap the photo. A bigger view opens above the grid.
3. To move it, pick another function under **Album**, then tap **Move**.
4. To delete it, tap **Delete photo**, then tap **Delete photo** again to confirm.
5. Tap **Close** when you are done.

**Tip:** Tap **Open** to see the photo on its own, or **Download** to save just that one.
**Stuck?** No **Move** or **Delete photo**? Helpers can change only their own photos. In a read-only wedding, nobody can change photos. A deleted photo is gone for everyone and can't come back.

## How to download everything
1. Open **Photos**.
2. Tap **Download all**. To get one function only, open its album and tap **Download album**.
3. Wait while it says "Counting photos…".
4. Tap each **Part** button, such as "Part 1 of 2". Each part is one ZIP file.
5. Keep the page open until each part finishes.

**Tip:** Do this on Wi-Fi from a computer if you can. Big galleries come in several parts, and phones get smaller parts.
**Stuck?** A part says it couldn't be downloaded? Tap the same part again.
