# Notifications and reminders

**What it is:** The bell, your inbox, your own reminders and an optional morning email, all in one place.
**Why you'd use it:** The haldi moves to 5 pm. You see it in the bell before Mausi calls to ask the new time.

**Who can see this:** Notices are only for you. Payment notices, and the payment line in the morning email, are only for hosts, planners and family. Helpers and viewers never get them. The morning email shows titles and counts, never amounts.

## How to read and clear your notices
1. Tap the bell at the top. Your newest notices open in a sheet.
2. Tap a notice. It is marked read and takes you to the right page.
3. Tap **Mark all read** to clear every unread dot at once.
4. Tap **See all notifications** to open your **Inbox**.
5. In the **Inbox**, tap **Clear read**, then **Clear**. Notices you already read are removed. Unread ones stay.

**Tip:** A number on the bell means unread notices. It turns red only when something is due today.
**Stuck?** If **Mark all read** is not there, you have no unread notices. If it fails, the wedding may be read-only, so ask a host.

## How to set a reminder for yourself
1. Tap the (+) button, then **Remind me**.
2. Under **What should we remind you about?**, type your note, like "Call the caterer about the menu".
3. Pick the **Date**.
4. Pick the **Time**. It starts at 09:00.
5. Tap **Add reminder**.
6. Your reminder shows under **Coming up**. At that time it lands in your **Inbox**, within a few minutes.

**Tip:** Only you see your reminders. They always arrive, even if you switch off other notices. To remove one, tap **Delete reminder**.
**Stuck?** If you see "Pick a time in the future.", choose a later time. If you see "Write what to remind you about", the note box is empty.

## How to choose what you're told about
1. Open **Notifications**, then tap the **Settings** tab. On a phone, **Notifications** is under **More**. Hosts and planners can also reach it from the bell.
2. Find the card **What should we tell you about?**
3. Tick what you want to hear about. Untick what you don't.
4. Tap **Save**. You will see "Saved."

**Tip:** You only see choices that fit your role. Payments are for hosts, planners and family. "Someone accepts an invite" is for hosts and planners.
**Stuck?** Some notices about your wedding's data and about Krama support always arrive for hosts and planners. You cannot switch those off.

## How to get the morning email
1. Open **Notifications**, then tap the **Settings** tab.
2. Scroll to the card called **Email**.
3. Pick **Every morning at 8:00**. Hosts can also pick **Mondays only**.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** The email is off until you switch it on. It comes from 8:00 in your wedding's time zone. It lists your tasks, functions in the next 48 hours and new replies. Hosts, planners and family also get a payments line. Hosts on **Mondays only** get one summary of the week ahead.
**Stuck?** No email on a day means nothing needed you. If you see "Email isn't set up yet", try again later. Anyone in the wedding can turn it on, but **Mondays only** is for hosts.

## How to stop the morning email
1. Open the latest morning email.
2. Tap the stop link at the bottom.
3. On the page that opens, tap **Stop the morning brief**.
4. You will see "Stopped". You can switch it on again in **Notifications**, then **Settings**.

**Tip:** You can also open **Notifications**, tap **Settings**, pick **Off** and tap **Save**. This stops it only for this wedding.
**Stuck?** "This link no longer works" means the link is old. Use the link in your latest email, or sign in and use **Settings**. If you see "That didn't go through", wait a few minutes and try again.
