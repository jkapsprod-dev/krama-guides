# Gold today

**What it is:** A small note under your jewellery budget line that shows today's gold price per gram.
**Why you'd use it:** Mama ji says gold has gone up since the engagement. You want a rough price in front of you before you set the jewellery budget.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family, because it sits on the **Budget** page. Helpers and viewers do not get Money.

**It is a guide price only.** It is the international gold price turned into rupees. A jeweller charges more. Import duty, GST and making charges come on top. Krama never adds this price to your budget.

## How to see today's gold price on your jewellery budget
1. Open **Money**, then tap the **Budget** tab.
2. Scroll to your jewellery line. In the standard list it is called **Attire and jewellery**.
3. Read the note under it. It starts with **Gold today** and gives the 24K and 22K price per gram.
4. Read the small line below it. It says the date the price is from.
5. Type your own amount for the line, then tap **Save budget**.

**Tip:** Use the note only to get a feel for the price. Ask your jeweller for the real rate before you decide.
**Stuck?** You do not see the note? It shows only under a budget line named with jewellery or gold in it. It is also hidden if the price could not load right now, or if the helper is off. Check **Smart helpers**.

## How to switch the gold price off
1. Open **More**, then **Settings**. On a computer, click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Tap **Smart helpers**.
3. Untick **Gold price on the jewellery budget line**.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** You can switch it back on the same way, any time.
**Stuck?** The box is greyed out? Only a host or a planner can change it. If it says **Switched off by Krama for now.** then Krama has paused it for everyone.
