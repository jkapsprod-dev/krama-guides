# Snap a bill

**What it is:** Take a photo of a bill and Krama fills in an expense for you to check.
**Why you'd use it:** The decorator hands you a paper bill at the venue. You do not want to type every line while the baraat is arriving.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family, the same people who can add an expense. Helpers and viewers never see it.

**Switched off to begin with:** Snap a bill is off for every wedding until a host or planner switches it on. Krama can also switch it off for everyone.

## How to switch Snap a bill on
1. Open **More**, then **Settings**. On a computer, click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Tap **Smart helpers**.
3. Tick **Snap a bill**.
4. Tap **Save**. You see **Saved.**

**Tip:** Only a host or a planner can do this. Ask one of them if the ticks are greyed out for you.
**Stuck?** The box is greyed out and says **Switched off by Krama for now.** Then Krama has paused this helper for everyone. Try again later.

## How to add an expense from a bill photo
1. Open **Money**, then tap **Add expense**. You can also tap the (+) and choose **Add an expense**.
2. Find the **Snap a bill** card at the top. Tap **Take or choose a photo**.
3. Take a photo of the bill, or choose one from your phone. Lay the bill flat and keep it clear.
4. Wait while it says **Reading the bill…**. The photo is read on your phone.
5. Check every box in the form. Krama may fill in **What is it for?**, **Vendor**, **Total agreed** and **Notes**. Change anything that is wrong.
6. Tap **Save expense**. Nothing is saved until you do this.

**Tip:** The notes can hold the bill number, the bill date and the supplier's GSTIN. Krama keeps the photo with the expense as its receipt.
**Stuck?** It says it could not find anything it trusts? Take a clearer, flatter photo, or fill the form in yourself. If you see **Send this photo for reading**, you can tap it to have the text read on Krama's side instead. Only the text comes back, and Krama does not keep that copy. The reader can make mistakes, so always check the total.
