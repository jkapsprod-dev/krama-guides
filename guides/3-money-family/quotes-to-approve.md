# Quotes to approve

**What it is:** The **Quotes** tab in **Money** lists the quotes your planner has sent you. A host picks one and approves it.
**Why you'd use it:** Your planner got quotes from two decorators for the mandap. You compare them in one place and approve the one your family likes.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family can open **Quotes** and see the prices. Only a host can approve a quote. Helpers and viewers never see it. Krama only records your choice. It does not hold, move or pay money.

## How to approve a quote your planner sent
1. Open **Money**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Tap the **Quotes** tab.
3. Find the job, like "Mandap decor". Each vendor's quote sits under it with its price.
4. Read each quote. Look at the **Includes** line and **Price holds until**.
5. Tap **Approve** next to the quote you want.
6. Read the question, then tap **Approve** again to confirm.

The vendor now shows **Approved**. They are added to your wedding, and the amount is added to your budget. The other quotes for that job are declined.

**Tip:** Check the **Includes** line before you choose. A lower price may cover less.
**Stuck?** If you do not see **Approve**, you are not signed in as a host. Ask a host to do it. Planners and family can read quotes but not approve them. If you see "That quote can’t be approved any more.", it was already approved or is no longer open. Refresh the page.

If you see "Nothing to approve.", your planner has not sent any quotes yet.
