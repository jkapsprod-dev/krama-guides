# Find my photos

**Status:** Planned. Not available yet.

**What it is:** A way for each guest to find the photos they appear in, by taking one selfie.
**Why you'd use it:** After the wedding, Mama ji wants only his own photos from the haldi. He should not have to scroll through 800 pictures to find them.

**Who can see this:** Hosts and planners will see a **Face search** card on the **Photos** screen. Only a host will switch it on. They will see only how many guests said yes, never who, and never which photos. Each guest chooses for themselves. Guests will see only their own photos. There is no money in this feature.

Today, if a host has switched face search on, guests who open the **Photos** tab see only the message "Find my photos is coming soon." Nothing is kept about anyone's face.

## How a host will switch on face search
1. Open **Photos**.
2. Find the **Face search** card.
3. Tap **Switch on face search**.
4. Check the count of guests who have said yes.

**Tip:** You can tap **Switch off** at any time. A confirm box tells you that every guest's face pattern is then deleted at once.
**Stuck?** No **Switch on face search** button? Only a host can switch it on, and it needs the face search add-on. Ask a host.

## How a guest will find their photos
1. Open your family's RSVP link and go to the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Find my photos**, tap **Find photos of** your name.
3. Read the short notice and tick the box to agree.
4. Tap **Take a selfie**.
5. Open the photos that were found for you.

**Tip:** Your photos show only on the phone or browser that took the selfie. Each person in a family agrees for themselves.
**Stuck?** You see "Find my photos is coming soon."? It is not ready yet. Check again later. You can tap **Stop face search** at any time, and everything about you is deleted at once.
