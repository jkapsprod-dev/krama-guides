# Money by side

**What it is:** The **Sides** tab in **Money** shows what the bride's family, the groom's family and both together have planned, booked and paid.
**Why you'd use it:** Before the sangeet, both fathers sit down to settle accounts. You print each family's page, so everyone sees the same numbers.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family see these totals. Helpers and viewers never see them. Krama only records the numbers. It does not hold, move or pay money.

## How to see one family's share
1. Open **Money**. On a phone, tap **More** first.
2. Tap the **Sides** tab.
3. Read the card **Money by side**. **Bride’s side**, **Groom’s side** and **Shared** each show **Planned**, **Booked**, **Paid** and **Still to pay**.
4. Tap a family's name to see that side's payments.

**Tip:** Anything you do not put on a family stays under **Shared**. To move a cost, change **Whose side** on the expense. You can also split a budget line in the **Budget** tab.
**Stuck?** If the card says everything is shared, no expense or budget line is on a family yet. Put an expense on a family's side, or split a budget line, and the numbers appear.

## How to print or download it
1. Open **Money**, then the **Sides** tab.
2. Under a family, tap **Print**. A page opens with that family's summary, expenses and what is still to pay.
3. Tap **Print** at the top of that page. Your phone or computer opens its print screen. You can also save it as a PDF.
4. To get a spreadsheet instead, tap **CSV** under a family. The page also has **Download CSV**.

**Tip:** Save the page as a PDF and share it yourself on WhatsApp. Krama does not send it for you.
**Stuck?** If the page says "No expenses on this side yet.", add an expense and set **Whose side** to that family.
