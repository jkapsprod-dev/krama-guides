# Vendors

**What it is:** One list of the caterer, decorator, photographer and everyone else you have booked, with their phone numbers and what you still owe them.
**Why you'd use it:** The caterer's advance is due on Friday. You want his number and his balance in one place, not in three WhatsApp chats.

**Who can see this:** Hosts, planners and family can open **Vendors**. They see names, phones and amounts. Helpers and viewers do not get the Money section at all.

## How to add a vendor
1. Open **Money**, then tap the **Vendors** tab.
2. Tap **Add vendor**.
3. Type the **Name**, for example Shree Caterers. Pick the **Type**, such as Caterer or Decor.
4. Type the **Phone**. Indian numbers need no +91. You can also fill in **Company**, **Email** and **Notes**.
5. Tap **Save vendor**.

**Tip:** You can also add a vendor while you add an expense. Choose **A new vendor…** in the **Vendor** box.
**Stuck?** You do not see **Add vendor**? Your wedding may be read-only. A read-only wedding lets you look but not change anything.

## How to find a vendor, call them and see what you owe
1. Open **Money**, then tap **Vendors**.
2. Look under each name. You see the type, then **Booked** with the amount, then what is due. It says **Fully paid** when nothing is left.
3. Tap the phone icon, called **Call {name}**, to ring the vendor from your phone.
4. Tap the chat icon, called **WhatsApp {name}**, to open WhatsApp with their number ready. You press send yourself.
5. Tap the vendor's name to open their page. Under **Expenses** you see each booking and how much is paid.

**Tip:** On a vendor's page, tap **Add expense for {name}** to add a new booking with that vendor already chosen.
**Stuck?** No phone icons beside a name? You did not save a phone number. Tap the name, tap **Edit**, add the **Phone** and tap **Save vendor**.

## How to show a vendor to guests as a contact
1. Open **Money**, tap **Vendors**, then tap the vendor's name.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Tick **Show to guests on their wedding page**. It is off until you tick it.
4. In **What guests see (e.g. Airport pickup)**, type what this person does for guests.
5. Tap **Save vendor**.

**Tip:** Guests then see this name and phone as a contact for the day. Use it for the person who handles pickups, not for every vendor.
**Stuck?** Changed your mind? Open the vendor, tap **Edit**, untick the box and tap **Save vendor**. Guests stop seeing the name and phone.
