# Commission earnings

**Time it saves:** Planned feature, so nothing to save yet.
**What it is:** The **Earnings** tab in **Money** for planner businesses. It is the place where commission from linked weddings would show.
**Why you'd use it:** Neha Desai brings families to Krama with her planner code, and wonders if she earns anything for it.
**Not live yet:** Commission is switched off and set to 0, and payouts wait for a review.

## What the screen shows today
- Only planner owners and admins see the **Earnings** tab, inside **Money**. Vendors do not have it.
- The page has a short note about commission and three totals: **Waiting**, **Approved** and **Paid out**.
- With no earnings, you see **No earnings yet**. The page suggests you share your planner code with families.
- Until there are earnings, all three totals show ₹0.
