# Leads

**Time it saves:** No more phone numbers lost in WhatsApp chats. Every person who asked about your work sits in one list, with the day you meant to call back.
**What it is:** A list of everyone who has asked about your work, from the first call to a booking.
**Why you'd use it:** A bride's mother rings about a December wedding while you are busy at a mandap. You note her number in a minute. On Monday, the follow-up date tells you to call her back.

**Who can use this:** Everyone in your business. Only owners and admins can start a proposal from a lead. In this guide you add leads by hand, from a call, a message or a visit.

## How to add a lead
1. Open **Leads**. On a phone, it sits under **More**.
2. Click **Add lead**. A sheet opens.
3. Type the **Name** and the **Phone**. These two are enough.
4. Pick the **Event date** if you know it. Under **Where did they come from?**, pick **Phone call**, **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** or another source.
5. Pick a **Follow up on** date if you want a reminder. Then click **Add lead**.

**Tip:** You can also click the **(+)** and choose **Add a lead**. A lead with a **Follow up** tag in the list is one whose date has come.
**Stuck?** The screen says the person already has an open lead with this phone number? Click **Open that lead** to use the old one, or **Add it anyway** if it really is a new enquiry. The empty list also mentions an enquiry form. That is not live yet, so add leads by hand with **Add lead**.

## How to move a lead through the stages
1. Open **Leads** and click the lead's name.
2. Find the **Stage** row. It shows **New**, **Contacted**, **Meeting / visit**, **Proposal sent**, **Negotiation**, **Won** and **Lost**.
3. Click the stage the lead has reached. The lead shows that stage in the list at once.
4. If the family says no, click **Lost**. Type the reason under **Why was it lost?**.
5. Click **Mark as lost**.

**Tip:** In the list, **Open** shows leads still in play. Click **All** to see **Won** and **Lost** too. The **Lead history** panel on the right shows how far the lead has come, from enquiry to invoices.
**Stuck?** The **Mark as lost** button does not work? It needs a reason. Type a few words first.

## How to note a call or a meeting
1. Open **Leads** and click the lead's name.
2. Scroll to **Notes and calls**.
3. Type what was said under **Add a note**. For example, "Wants haldi in a garden, 150 guests."
4. Under **Type**, pick **Note**, **Call**, **Message** or **Meeting**.
5. Click **Add note**.

**Tip:** Change the **Follow up on** date in **Details** after each call, and click **Save**. Then the lead comes back to you on the right day.
**Stuck?** A warning says that date is full or blocked? That is a warning, not a block. Check the **Calendar** before you promise the date.

## How to turn a lead into a client
1. Open **Leads** and click the lead's name.
2. Click **Make a client**.
3. The client page opens. The lead's name is the client, and the lead's person is added under **People** as the one who **Decides**.
4. Add the others who pay or decide with **Add person**.

**Tip:** After this, the button on the lead says **Open client**. It takes you straight to the same client.
**Stuck?** Made a client by mistake? Open the client and click **Delete client**. The lead stays.
