# Subscription

**Time it saves:** Planned feature, so nothing to save yet.
**What it is:** A page in your business **Settings** that shows where your Krama plan stands.
**Why you'd use it:** Your accountant asks if Utsav Planners pays for Krama. You open this page and see the answer.
**Not live yet:** Business plans are off sale, so there is nothing to subscribe to.

## What the screen shows today
- Open **Settings**, then click **Subscription** at the top of the page.
- The page is called **Subscription**. It says "Your Krama plan, renewals and invoices."
- The status reads **Free during the pilot**. A line under it says this is how every business uses Krama while it is in its pilot.
- In the **Subscribe** box, you see "Nothing to pay during the pilot."
- The **Invoices** box says "No invoices yet."
- Owners and admins reach it from **Settings**. Only the owner can subscribe, and subscribing is switched off today.
