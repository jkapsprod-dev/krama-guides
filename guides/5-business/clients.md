# Clients

**Time it saves:** One page per family, with every phone number and who decides. No more asking "who do I call about the payment?"
**What it is:** The families and companies you work with, and the people you deal with in each one.
**Why you'd use it:** The bride's father pays, the bride picks the decor, and her cousin is at the venue on the day. You note who is who once, and your whole team can see it.

**Who can use this:** Everyone in your business.

## How to add a client
1. Open **Clients**. On a phone, it sits under **More**.
2. Click **Add client**.
3. Type the **Family or company name**. For example, "Shah family".
4. Under **Type**, pick **Family** or **Company**. Add **Notes** if you like.
5. Click **Add client**. The client's page opens.

**Tip:** Most clients come from a lead. Open the lead and click **Make a client**, and the name and phone are filled in for you.
**Stuck?** The name is empty? **Family or company name** must be filled in before you can add the client.

## How to add the people who decide and pay
1. Open **Clients** and click the client's name.
2. Under **People**, type the **Name**, **Phone** and **Email** of the person.
3. Tick **Decides** for the person who makes the choices.
4. Tick **Pays** for the person who settles the bills. Tick **Day-of contact** for the person who picks up the phone on the wedding day.
5. Click **Add person**.

**Tip:** One person can have all three ticks. Each tick shows as a small tag next to their name, so your crew sees it at a glance.
**Stuck?** Added the wrong person? Click **Remove** next to their name and confirm. Then add them again.
