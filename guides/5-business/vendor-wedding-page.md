# Your wedding page (vendors)

**Time it saves:** The brief, the timings, your crew and your to-do lines sit on one page, not in five WhatsApp chats.
**What it is:** For each wedding you are linked to, one page with only what you need: the brief, the functions, your tools, your crew and your deliverables.
**Why you'd use it:** The decor vendor must know the haldi setup time. The planner changes the brief at night, and you see the change in the morning.

You reach this page from **Weddings** in the sidebar, or from **Today's jobs** on Home. Staff do not see money on this page. Owners and admins also get a **Money** section with your own payments.

## How to read the brief
1. Open **Weddings** and click the wedding.
2. If a brief is new or changed, a banner says "A brief is waiting for you" or "The brief has changed". **What changed** shows added and removed lines.
3. Read the **Brief**, and the name of who to call on the day.
4. Click **I have read this**. The page says "You have read version" and its number.

**Tip:** Use **Brief** and the other tabs under the title to jump down the page.
**Stuck?** If "The brief changed again. Read it, then acknowledge." shows, read the page again and click **I have read this** once more. "No brief yet." means the family or planner has not written one.

## How to mark the day
1. Open the wedding and go to **Functions**.
2. Find the function you work on. You see its date, time and venue. **Open the map** and **Call sheet** are links there too.
3. On the day, click **Arrived** when your team reaches the venue.
4. Click **Set up done** when your setup is ready, and **Finished** when you are done.

**Tip:** Each button shows its time once pressed. Pressed by mistake? Click the lit button again to take it back.
**Stuck?** No functions? The family has not added any yet. The dates come from them.

## How to use your tools
1. Open the wedding and go to **Your tools**.
2. Pick a list. Your lists depend on your trade. Everyone has a **Checklist**. A photographer also has a **Shot list**, a caterer a **Menu**, a DJ a **Song list**, and a decor vendor a **Bill of quantities** and **Equipment**.
3. First time on a **Checklist**? Click **Add a starter checklist** to get lines for your trade, with due dates.
4. To add a line, type it in the field, such as **Add a task**, then click **Add**.
5. Tick **Done** when a line is finished. Click **Remove** to take it off.

**Tip:** The family never sees your lists. Use them freely, even for small reminders.
**Stuck?** No **Add a starter checklist** button? It shows only until you have one task. Your trade decides which lists you see.

## How to add crew and deliverables
1. Open the wedding and go to **Your crew**.
2. Type the name in **Crew member** and their job in **Role**. Choose a **Function**, or leave **All functions**. Click **Add crew**.
3. Go to **Deliverables**. Type what you will deliver in **What will you deliver?**, and a **Due date**.
4. Click **Add deliverable**.
5. Change the status to **To do**, **In progress** or **Delivered** as you go.

**Tip:** Overdue deliverables show on your Home under **Needs your attention**. Mark them **Delivered** to clear them.
**Stuck?** To fix a mistake, use **Remove** beside a crew member or **Delete** beside a deliverable. Both ask you to confirm first.
