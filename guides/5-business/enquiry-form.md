# Enquiry form

**Time it saves:** Not live yet, so nothing to save today. Add your leads by hand with [[leads]].
**What it is:** A page with your business name on it, where a family can write to you about their wedding, with the answers going into your lead list.
**Why you'd use it:** A cousin sees your Instagram and wants to ask about a December mehndi. A form on your bio link would save you typing her details from a message.

**Not live yet:** The security check that stops fake enquiries is not switched on for the live site, so the form cannot be sent.

## What the screen shows today
- In **Settings**, owners and admins see a card called **Enquiry form**. It has a switch, **Accept enquiries through the form**.
- When the switch is on, the card shows a link and a **Copy link** button.
- The link opens a page with your business name and these fields: **Name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Event date**, **City** and "Anything else we should know?". Its button is **Send enquiry**.
- That button does not work yet, because of the security check. Please do not share the link with anyone for now.
