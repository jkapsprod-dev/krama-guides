# Crew and shifts

**Time it saves:** You write each person's call time once, and the message is ready to send, so there is no retyping for every crew member.
**What it is:** A list of your crew, the shifts you give them, and a call sheet for each day that you send from your own WhatsApp.
**Why you'd use it:** At 6 am on the wedding day, your tent crew needs to know where to be and when, and you do not want to type it eight times.

## How to add crew
Anyone on the team can add crew. They do not need an account.

1. Open **Calendar**, then the **Crew** tab.
2. Click **Add crew**.
3. Type the **Name** and **Phone**.
4. Pick the **Type**: **Freelancer** or **Staff**. Add their **Skills**.
5. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** **Day rate (₹)** shows only to owners and admins. Staff never see it. If someone is away, click **Pause**. They stay on the list but cannot be given shifts. Click **Resume** when they are back.
**Stuck?** If it says "That doesn't look like a phone number.", type the number with the area or country code.

## How to give someone a shift
1. Open **Calendar**, then the **Crew** tab.
2. Scroll to **Assign to a function**.
3. Pick **Who** and **Which function**. The list shows the functions of weddings you are linked to. For your own job, pick **Something else (enter times)** and fill the date, times and venue.
4. Add a **Role** and a **Note for them**, such as "Bring extension boards."
5. Click **Assign**. You see "Assigned."

If the person is already booked then, you see "Already booked then:" and the name of the other job. The shift is still saved. To take a shift off, click **Remove shift** under **Coming up**.

**Tip:** Put the reporting time in **Note for them**. It goes into the message on the call sheet.
**Stuck?** **Assign to a function** shows only when you have at least one active crew member. Add crew first. Paused people do not show.

## How to send the call sheet
1. Open **Calendar**, then the **Crew** tab.
2. Under **Coming up**, find "Call sheets:" and click the date you want.
3. Check the sheet. It lists each function, the venue, who is on it and the times.
4. Click **Open in WhatsApp** next to a name. Your own WhatsApp opens with the message ready.
5. Press send in WhatsApp.

Krama does not send anything by itself. You press send. To paste the message somewhere else, click **Copy message**. To print the sheet, click **Print or save as PDF**.

**Tip:** If a person has a private shift link, the message ends with "Your shifts:" and their link.
**Stuck?** If WhatsApp asks who to send to, that person has no phone saved. Go back to **Crew**, click **Edit** and add their **Phone**.

## How to give crew a private shift link
Owners and admins only.

1. Open **Calendar**, then the **Crew** tab.
2. Find the person and click **Phone access**.
3. Under **Private shift link**, click **Make link**.
4. Click **Copy link**.
5. Send the link to them yourself, for example on WhatsApp.

The link shows only their own coming shifts. It has no rates and nothing about guests. Anyone who has the link can open it, so send it to one person only.

**Tip:** If a link goes to the wrong person, click **New link**. The old link stops working at once. **Turn off** stops it for good, and you can make a new one later.
**Stuck?** If the person says "This link doesn't work", the link was replaced or turned off. Send them the new one.

## How to see your own shifts (crew)
If you got a private link, open it on your phone. You do not need to sign in. The page is called **Your shifts**.

If you have an account, sign in and follow these steps.

1. Open **Calendar**.
2. Click the **My shifts** tab.
3. Read your shifts, grouped by day, with the time, the role and the note.
4. Tap the venue name to open it in Maps.

**Tip:** Owners and admins link an account to a crew name. They open **Phone access**, pick the person under **Login** and click **Save login**.
**Stuck?** If it says "Your login isn't on the crew list yet", ask an owner or admin to open **Phone access** for your name and link your login.
