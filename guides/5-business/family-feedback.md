# Family feedback

**Time it saves:** Every family's rating and note is in one place, so you do not scroll old chats to remember how a wedding went.
**What it is:** A private page where a planner reads the ratings and notes that families send after their wedding.
**Why you'd use it:** Before you meet a new client, you want to know how the Sharma-Patel family felt about your team.

Planner owners and admins only. Staff and vendors do not see this page.

## How to read families' ratings
1. Open **Home**.
2. Click **Family feedback**, the button at the top right.
3. Read the line that starts with "Average". It shows your average out of 5 and how many families rated you.
4. Read each row. It shows the Wedding ID, the rating, the family's note and the date.
5. Click **Back to your dashboard** when you are done.

Families send feedback from their Wrap-up, after the last function. It is private. It is never shown in public. It stays with your business even after the family deletes their wedding. A family can change or withdraw its rating, so the page can change.

**Tip:** Green means 4 or 5, amber means 3 and red means 1 or 2. Read the red ones first. They tell you the most.
**Stuck?** If it says "No feedback yet", no family has rated you. Families can rate you after the last function of their wedding. If you do not see the **Family feedback** button, you are not an owner or admin.
