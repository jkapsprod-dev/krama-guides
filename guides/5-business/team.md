# Team

**Time it saves:** You see who has access to your weddings in one short list, and you can take access away in two clicks.
**What it is:** A list of the people in your business and their roles, with buttons to remove someone or to leave.
**Why you'd use it:** A freelancer who ran the sangeet desk has moved on, and you want them off every wedding today.

## How to see your team and roles
1. Open **Team**. On a phone it sits under **More**.
2. Read the list. Each person has a role under their name: **Owner**, **Admin** or **Staff**.
3. Read the note at the top. Everyone on the team sees every wedding your business is linked to.

**Tip:** On **Home**, the **Team** card has a **See team** link that opens the same list.
**Stuck?** If a name shows as "Team member", that person has not added their name yet. The role next to it is still right.

## How to remove someone
Owners and admins only.

1. Open **Team**.
2. Find the person and click **Remove**.
3. Read the message. It says they lose access to every linked wedding straight away.
4. Click **Remove** again to confirm.

**Tip:** Only an owner can remove another owner. An admin can remove admins and staff.
**Stuck?** No **Remove** button next to a name? You may not be an owner or admin. Or the person is the only owner, and a business must keep one owner.

## How to leave the business
Anyone on the team can leave.

1. Open **Team**.
2. Find your own name and click **Leave business**.
3. Read the message. You lose access to the weddings straight away.
4. Click **Leave business** again to confirm. You go back to your list of businesses.

**Tip:** Leaving takes you off this business only. It does not delete your Krama account.
**Stuck?** The only owner has no **Leave business** button, because a business needs at least one owner.
