# Business Home

**Time it saves:** One screen shows what needs you today, so you do not open each wedding to check.
**What it is:** The first page of your business. A planner sees what needs them and how each wedding is doing. A vendor sees today's jobs and what is waiting.
**Why you'd use it:** You wake up on the morning of the sangeet and want to know, in one look, which wedding has a problem.

## How to work through Needs you
Planners only. Vendors have **Needs your attention** instead (see the next task).
1. Open **Home**. The numbered list called **Needs you** is at the top. The worst problem is first.
2. Read the line. It says what is wrong, for example "Follow up with {name}" or "{count} tasks are overdue".
3. Click the line. It opens the lead, the wedding or the invoice that needs you.
4. Fix it, then come back to **Home** for the next line.

**Tip:** A red number means it is late. The word at the right end of the line, such as **Leads**, **Weddings** or **Money**, tells you where it will take you.
**Stuck?** "Nothing needs you right now." is good news. Staff do not see money lines, so their list can be shorter.

## How to read Your weddings or Today's jobs
1. Open **Home**.
2. Planners: look at **Your weddings**. Each wedding has one row, with its next function, overdue tasks, RSVPs waiting, approvals, pickups and rooms.
3. Vendors: look at **Today's jobs** for weddings with a function today. Below it, **Needs your attention** lists a brief to read, a quote request, open deliverables and overdue payments.
4. Look for the coloured tags. Words come with every colour, so you can read them without the colour.
5. Scroll down for **Coming up**, **Waiting for approval** and **Team**.

**Tip:** On a wide screen, **Your weddings** becomes a table with one column per area, so you can scan down the column that matters.
**Stuck?** "No weddings yet" means no wedding is linked. Open **Weddings** and ask for one by its ID. Vendors see **Linked weddings** too, and the amount overdue to you shows for owners and admins only.

## How to open a wedding
1. Planners: click the wedding's row in **Your weddings**. The wedding opens in the wedding app, with your planner access.
2. Vendors: click the wedding in **Today's jobs** or **Needs your attention**. Your page for that wedding opens.
3. When a client accepts your proposal, a line in **Needs you** ends with **Open wedding**. Click it to go to the new wedding.
4. To come back, use **Switch account** and choose your business.

**Tip:** Your Needs you lines are links too, so you can reach most weddings from the first screen.
**Stuck?** The wedding does not open? Your link may have ended. Check **Weddings** for **Waiting for approval**.
