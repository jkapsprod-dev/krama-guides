# Set up your business

**Time it saves:** One ID to share with every family, instead of explaining who you are on each WhatsApp thread.
**What it is:** Your business gets its own page in Krama, with its own ID, for the weddings you plan or serve.
**Why you'd use it:** A family asks "how do we add you to our wedding?" and you send one ID, not a long message.

## How to set up a planner or vendor business
1. Sign in and open **Your weddings**. At the bottom, under **Run a wedding business?**, click **Set up your business**. If you have only one wedding, Krama opens it at once. Click **Your weddings** at the top of the sidebar to see the list.
2. Under **What kind of business?**, choose **Wedding planner** or **Vendor**.
3. Type your **Business name**. Add your city in **City (optional)** if you like.
4. Vendors only: pick your trade in **What do you do?**, for example **Photography** or **Décor & tent**.
5. Click **Set up business**. Your business Home opens.

**Tip:** Already have a business? Krama opens it straight away. With two or more businesses, Krama shows **Your businesses** first, and **Set up another business** adds one more.
**Stuck?** Your trade is fixed once you set up, so pick it with care. If the button says new businesses are paused, try again a little later.

## How to find and share your Business ID
Owners and admins only.
1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Find the box called **Business ID**. A planner's ID starts with P-. A vendor's starts with V-.
3. Click **Copy**.
4. Paste it into WhatsApp or an email to the family or planner you work with.

**Tip:** You can also read your ID under your business name at the top of the sidebar. Share it only with people you trust. The ID alone gives no one access.
**Stuck?** If **Settings** says the page is not found, you are signed in as Staff. Ask an owner or admin to send you the ID.

## How to switch between your wedding and your business
1. Look for **Switch account** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone, tap your business name at the top.
2. Under **Weddings**, choose a wedding. Under **Businesses**, choose a business.
3. The sheet closes and that account opens.

**Tip:** The account you are in is shown in bold in the sheet.
**Stuck?** **Switch account** shows only when you have a wedding and a business, or two businesses. With just one account there is nothing to switch to.
