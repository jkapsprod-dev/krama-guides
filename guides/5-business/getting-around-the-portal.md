# Getting around the portal

**Time it saves:** Start a lead, a proposal or an invoice from anywhere, without hunting through the menu.
**What it is:** Three shortcuts at the top and bottom of every business page: the (+), search and the inbox bell.
**Why you'd use it:** You are at a venue and a client says "send me the invoice today". You open the (+) and start it in two taps.

## How to use the (+)
The (+) is on phones and narrow windows. On a wide screen, use search instead (next task).
1. Tap the round (+) in the middle of the bottom bar. It is named **Add**.
2. Pick what you want to start: **Add a lead**, **Start a proposal**, **Payment received**, **Raise an invoice**, **Make a challan** or **Block time on the calendar**.
3. The right page opens, ready to fill in.

**Tip:** Vendors also see **Add a tool item**. Staff see only **Make a challan**, **Block time on the calendar** and, for vendors, **Add a tool item**. Money rows are for owners and admins.
**Stuck?** No (+) on your screen? Make the window narrower, or click search and look under **Actions**.

## How to search pages and actions
1. Click **Search pages and actions** at the top of the page. On a phone, tap the search icon. You can also press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
2. Start typing, or just look at the list. **Jump to** lists your pages. **Actions** lists what you can start.
3. Click a line. That page or form opens.

**Tip:** Search finds pages and actions only. It does not look inside your clients or leads.
**Stuck?** If it says "Nothing found", try part of a page name, such as "Leads" or "Calendar".

## How to read your inbox
1. Click the bell at the top right. It is named **Inbox**, and a number shows how many notes are new.
2. Read the notes. New ones are bold and marked "new". They tell you when a brief changes, a function moves, a function runs late, or a quote needs you.
3. Click a note to open the page it is about.
4. Click **Mark all read** to clear the new marks.

**Tip:** The **Weddings** page also has a **Notifications** button that opens the same inbox.
**Stuck?** "Nothing new." means there is nothing for you right now.
