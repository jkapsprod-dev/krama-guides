# First reply

**Time it saves:** Not live yet, so nothing to save today. Reply to your leads yourself from [[leads]].
**What it is:** One email in your own words, with your brochure, that goes to a person right after they send your enquiry form.
**Why you'd use it:** A family writes to you at midnight. A first reply with your brochure would reach them before you wake up, and you would answer in the morning.

**Not live yet:** It only answers enquiries from the enquiry form, and that form cannot be sent yet.

## What the screen shows today
- In **Settings**, owners and admins see a card called **First reply**.
- The card says this works only for people who send your enquiry form with an email address. While the form is off, it says "The enquiry form above is off, so nobody can reach this yet."
- It has a switch, **Send an automatic first reply**, and boxes for **Replies go to**, **Subject** and **Your message**. The message hint says to put {name} where the person's first name should go.
- Under **Brochure (PDF)** you can pick a brochure or click **Upload a new brochure**.
- A **Preview** shows how the email reads. The **Save** button keeps your changes.
- Nothing is sent today, because the enquiry form cannot be sent.
