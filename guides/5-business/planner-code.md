# Planner code

**Time it saves:** The family types one short code, so you do not read out a long ID over the phone.
**What it is:** A short code, like UTSAV-WEDDINGS, that a family uses to link you as their planner. The host always says yes before you see anything.
**Why you'd use it:** A bride's mother says "tell me how to add you" while you are on a site visit. You say the code, and she types it herself.

## How to choose your planner code
Planner businesses only. Owners and admins only.
1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Find the box called **Planner code**.
3. Type a code in **Choose a code**. Use 4 to 20 letters or numbers. Hyphens can separate parts, for example UTSAV-WEDDINGS.
4. Click **Save code**. You see "Saved:" and your code.
5. Click **Copy code**, then send the code to your families.

**Tip:** You get one free change later. Type a new one in **New code**, click **Change code**, then **Yes, change it**. The old code still works for 30 days.
**Stuck?** "That code isn't available. Try another." Someone else has it, so add your city or a number. After your free change, only Krama support can change it again.

## How to link a planner (family side)
Hosts and family. A host's link is active at once. A family member's request waits for a host.
1. Open **Codes & planner**. On a computer, go to **Settings** and then **Codes & planner**. On a phone, tap **More** and then **Codes & planner**.
2. Find the box called **Planner**. Under **Have a planner code?**, type the code your planner gave you.
3. Click **Find**. You see the business name and its ID.
4. Check it is the right planner, then click **Link planner**.
5. A host sees "Planner linked." A family member sees "Request sent. A host will see it here and decide."

**Tip:** Not sure it is the right planner? Ask them for their Business ID, which starts with P-, and compare.
**Stuck?** "No planner has that code. Check it with your planner." Ask them to read the code again. A wedding has only one planner, so remove the current one before you link another.

## How to approve or remove a planner (host side)
Hosts only.
1. Open **Codes & planner**, as in the task above.
2. Under **Planner**, look for a business with the tag **Waiting**. You see its name and ID.
3. Click **Approve** to let the planner in. Click **Refuse** to say no. Nobody sees anything until you approve.
4. To end a link later, click **Remove planner**, then **Yes, remove**. The planner loses access at once. You can link them again.

**Tip:** A planner who asked with your Wedding ID, and a planner your family added with a code, both wait here.
**Stuck?** Family members see "Waiting for a host to approve." Only a host has the buttons. If another planner is already linked, you see "Remove the current planner first to approve another."
