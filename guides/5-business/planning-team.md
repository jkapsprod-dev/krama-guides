# Planning team

**Time it saves:** You decide in one place who on your team sees the family's money, so you never have to explain why a junior saw the budget.
**What it is:** A panel on the wedding's **Members** page. It lists everyone from your planner business who works on this wedding, and says who is a planner, who is a coordinator, and who leads.
**Why you'd use it:** Your new coordinator runs the haldi desk. She needs the run sheet and her tasks, but not the family's budget.

The panel shows only when a planner business is linked to the wedding. Hosts and planners can see it. A host of the wedding, or an owner or admin of the planner business, can change it.

## How to make someone a coordinator or a planner
1. Open the wedding. Open **Members**. On a phone it sits under **More**.
2. Find **Planning team**. Everyone on your business's **Team** is listed with their role: **Planner** or **Coordinator**.
3. Next to the person, click **Make coordinator** or **Make planner**.
4. Read the message, then click the same button again to confirm.

**Tip:** A planner sees everything in the wedding, including money. A coordinator helps on the day, never sees money, and sees only what they are given tasks for.
**Stuck?** Someone from your business is missing? They join the panel when they are on your business's **Team**. See [[team]].

## How to choose who leads
1. Open **Members** and find **Planning team**.
2. Next to the person who runs this wedding, click **Make lead**.
3. Check that the **Lead** badge now sits next to their name.

**Tip:** Make the person the family calls first the lead. The family sees who is in charge at a glance.
**Stuck?** No buttons next to the names? Only a host of the wedding, or an owner or admin of your business, can change roles.
