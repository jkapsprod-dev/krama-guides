## Check these words

The AI voice spelled these by rule, so they may sound a little off: **mehta**, **kamra**, **stay**, **waiting**, **aasaan**. Add them to `kit\voice\hinglish-words.json` and run make again.

## Record your own voice for this video

Read the **Voice says** lines in order, calmly, with about one second of quiet between lines. Save it as `voiceover\my-voice\V-2-stays-and-rooms-3.wav`, then run `npm run make -- --id V-2-stays-and-rooms-3` in `kit\`. The kit cuts it into lines, the robot records again so every step fits your voice, and both videos are made again with your voice.
